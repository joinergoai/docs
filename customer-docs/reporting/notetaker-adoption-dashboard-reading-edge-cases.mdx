The **Notetaker Stats** card shows how many external meetings each person recorded. It is not part of Reporting.

## Who can use this

- Only the organization **Owner** and team **Admins** see the card.
- The card shows only if Ergo has turned it on for your organization.
- You cannot share the card.

## Steps

1. Select **Dashboard** in the left navigation.
2. Find the **Notetaker Stats** card at the top of the page.
3. Select **7d** or **30d**.
4. Read each team row: recorded meetings, external meetings, and the percentage.
5. Select a team row to see each person: **External Mtgs**, **Recorded**, **Bot**, **Desktop**, **% Recorded**.
6. For a person with **Not connected**, examine the calendar and notetaker setup of that person.
7. Before you coach a person about one meeting, open that meeting in **Meetings**.

## What to expect

- No other time range is available. The card has no user filter and no team filter.
- Teams show with the lowest percentage first. A person without a team shows below **Unassigned**.
- The card counts external meetings only. Internal meetings are not in the numbers.
- The card counts only meetings that the Ergo Notetaker (**Bot**) or the Ergo desktop app (**Desktop**) recorded.
- A meeting that only a third-party notetaker or an import captured does not count as recorded. A low percentage does not always show that a person had no recording.
- A person shows **Not connected**, with no numbers, when the calendar is not connected or no notetaker is on.
- Accounts with "test" in the email address do not show.

## Common issues

- A low percentage for one person: check if that person uses another notetaker.
- A recent meeting is missing: it may not be processed yet. See [Meeting processing time and status states](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3507520478).
- Do not treat the card as a full attendance or compliance record.
