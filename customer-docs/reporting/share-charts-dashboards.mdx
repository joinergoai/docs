You can share a Reporting dashboard with a link. You cannot share one chart with a link.

## Who can use this

- People who can create dashboards in Reporting.

## Before you start

- Viewers of a shared link can open the quotes and the deals behind each chart. Share only with people who can see that data.
- Your organization can turn off public links. The dialog then shows **Public links are turned off for your organization**.

## Steps

1. Click **Reporting** in the left navigation and open the dashboard.
2. Click the **⋯** menu next to the dashboard name.
3. Click **Share**.
4. Turn on **Share with a link**.
5. Click **Copy link**.
6. If the audience needs a password, turn on **Require a password**, type the password, and click **Done**.
7. Send the link (and the password) to the viewers. Tell them that Ergo asks for an email verification code.
8. To stop access, open **Share** again and turn off **Share with a link**.

## What to expect

- The link first shows **Verify Your Email**. The viewer types an email address, clicks **Send verification code**, and types the 6-digit code from the email. Then the password, if you set one.
- A viewer cannot export a CSV, edit a chart or change a grade. To open a call or a deal, the viewer must sign in to Ergo. Forecast charts do not show on a shared link.
- To send one chart, click **⋯** on the chart → **Download image**.
- When you turn off **Share with a link**, the link stops at once and Ergo deletes the password. If you turn it on again, Ergo makes a new link.

## Common issues

- No **Share** item: you do not have create access in Reporting.
- The viewer did not get the code: ask them to check spam, and to use the address that you expect.
- Passwords must have 6 to 128 characters: letters A–Z, numbers and common symbols, with no space at the start or the end.
