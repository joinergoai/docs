The organization **Owner** does this guide first. It follows the setup wizard, one step at a time. When you finish, your reps can do the [Rep onboarding guide](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/9716285693).

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner**.
- A team **Admin** gets a shorter wizard. See **If you are a team Admin** at the end of this guide.

## Before you start

- Have an admin login for your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce or Attio).
- Have your work email account (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365).
- If you connect Slack, you must be allowed to add apps to your Slack workspace.

## Start: sign in

- Go to dashboard.joinergo.com and sign in with Google, Microsoft or your work email.
- Click **Begin**. The left side lists the steps. **Skip step** passes a step that you do not need now.

## Step 1: Connect your CRM

- Click **Connect** next to HubSpot, Salesforce or Attio. Approve the connection in your CRM.
- The row then shows **Connected**.

![Step 1, Connect your CRM, with HubSpot, Salesforce and Attio.](../images/onboarding-admin-crm.png)

## Step 2: Connect your email and calendar

- Click **Connect** next to Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, and approve.
- The button changes to **Disconnect**. Each person connects their own email and calendar.

![Step 2, Connect your email and calendar.](../images/onboarding-admin-email.png)

## Step 3: Connect Slack

- Click **Connect** next to **Slack workspace** and approve in Slack.
- In **Enable Slack Monitoring**, click **Enable** to let Ergo update your CRM from your Slack channels, or click **Skip**.
- Click **Connect** next to **Your Slack account**. Ergo sends you call prep and approvals as a Slack DM.

![Step 3, the Slack workspace row and the Your Slack account row.](../images/onboarding-admin-slack.png)

## Step 4: Set up your notetaker

- For the Ergo Notetaker, connect your calendar first (step 2). Then click **Connect** and confirm.
- For a different notetaker, click **Connect** on its row. The window asks for a key or a token.
- One person can use one notetaker. The Ergo Notetaker and Ergo Desktop can go together.

![Step 4, Set up your notetaker, with the Ergo Notetaker and other notetakers.](../images/onboarding-admin-notetaker.png)

## Step 5: Connect your dialer

- If your team uses one of these dialers, click **Connect**. Outreach asks you to approve the connection. The other dialers ask for a key.
- If not, click **Skip step**.

![Step 5, Connect your dialer.](../images/onboarding-admin-dialer.png)

## Steps 6 and 7: Company details and CRM properties

- You can skip **Company details** (step 6) and fill it in later in **Settings**.
- In **CRM properties** (step 7), click the fields that Ergo must fill, then click **Sync Selected Properties**. **Custom** shows your own fields. **CRM Defined** shows the standard fields.
- Open each field and check its **Description** and **Write behavior**. **Amount** and **Close Date** start as **Never writes**.
- **Add Property** makes a new field. **Push to CRM** adds it to your CRM.

![The Select Properties to Sync from CRM window.](../images/onboarding-admin-properties.png)

## Step 8: CRM stages

- In **CRM Pipeline Stages**, select the stages that your team uses and click **Sync Stages**.
- **Pipeline Stages Control** opens next. An **Entry Point** is a stage where Ergo puts a new deal. Each pipeline needs one. Without it, Ergo does not create new deals.
- **Lock Stage** locks a stage. Ergo does not move a deal out of a locked stage.
- Click **Save Changes**. Then open each stage and write its **Description**.

![The CRM Pipeline Stages window.](../images/onboarding-admin-stages-sync.png)

![The Pipeline Stages Control window with Entry Point, Lock Stage and Status.](../images/onboarding-admin-stages-control.png)

## Step 9: Advanced settings

- **Pricing**: describe how you charge customers. Ergo uses it to get deal amounts right.
- **Domains**: add the other domains of your company and the domains of your partners.
- If you see **Qualification**, choose what Ergo checks before it creates a deal.

![Step 9, Advanced settings, on the Pricing tab.](../images/onboarding-admin-advanced.png)

## Step 10: Deal and account health

- The built-in signals have recommended weights. Change a weight if a signal matters more or less for your deals.
- **Add Signal** adds a signal of your own.

![Step 10, Deal and account health.](../images/onboarding-admin-health.png)

## Step 11: Email templates

- Click **Create template** and make one post-call email template for your team.
- Members use the templates. Admins edit them.

![Step 11, Email templates.](../images/onboarding-admin-templates.png)

## Step 12: Knowledge base

- Upload documents that Ergo can use when it drafts and answers.
- **My documents**, **Team** and **Organization** set who uses a document.

![Step 12, Knowledge base.](../images/onboarding-admin-kb.png)

## Step 13: Add your team

- Click **Add Member**. Enter the work email and the name. Select the team, the role and the seat. Click **Add person**.
- Ergo does not send an invite email. Tell each person to sign in at dashboard.joinergo.com and to read the [Rep onboarding guide](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/9716285693).

![Step 13, Add your team, with the Add Member button.](../images/onboarding-admin-team.png)

## Step 14: Automations

- To use a template, click **Use template**, set its options, and click **Create automation**. The automation is on at once.
- To build your own, click **Create automation** at the top right. Set **When**, **What**, **Where** and **Who can manage this**.

![Step 14, Automations, with the template cards.](../images/onboarding-admin-automations.png)

## Step 15: Configure reporting

- In **Reporting properties**, add the fields that you want to track: click **Add Default** or **Create**.
- In **Coaching rubrics**, choose the rubrics for your organization and teams.
- If your wizard has no step 15, your organization does not have Reporting.

![Step 15, Configure reporting.](../images/onboarding-admin-reporting.png)

## Finish: close the wizard

- Click **Finish**. The wizard closes, and it does not open again. Make later changes in **Settings**.
- A check mark on a step shows that you clicked **Next**. It does not show that a tool is connected.

![The Finish button at the end of the wizard.](../images/onboarding-admin-finish.png)

## After Finish

1. Make sure that each rep signs in and does the [Rep onboarding guide](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/9716285693).
2. Record one external meeting as a test. Then check the summary in **Meetings**, the draft in **Drafts** and the update in your CRM.
3. If a connection is missing, the **Dashboard** shows the card **Get Ergo working**. Click the button in each row.

## If you are a team Admin

Your wizard has the Owner steps without **Connect your CRM** and **Connect your dialer**. The Owner connects those for the organization.

- You do: email and calendar, Slack, notetaker, company details, CRM properties, CRM stages, advanced settings (if shown), deal and account health, email templates, knowledge base, add your team, automations and reporting (if shown).
- In **Add your team**, you add people to the teams that you manage.
- CRM properties and CRM stages apply to your team. Select your team at the top right of each page.

## Common issues

- No new deals are created: a pipeline has no **Entry Point**. Open **Settings → CRM Stages → Stage Settings** and turn one on.
- You skipped a step: open **Settings → Org integrations** (shared tools) or **Settings → User integrations** (your own tools).
- A rep sees no CRM step: this is correct. Only the Owner and team Admins set up the CRM.
