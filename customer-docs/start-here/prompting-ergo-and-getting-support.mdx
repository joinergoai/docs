Use this page to get better answers from Ergo. It also shows how to contact Ergo support.

## Who can use this

- Everyone can chat about one meeting on its page and can use **Support**.
- **Chat with Ergo** and the **Ask Ergo** panel on other pages need a **Standard** seat.
- A **Notetaker** seat gets the **Ask Ergo** panel on a meeting page only.

## Where to ask

- For a full-page chat, click **Chat with Ergo** in the left navigation.
- For a quick question about the page that you are on, click the round Ergo button at the bottom right. The **Ask Ergo** panel opens. You can also press Cmd+J (Mac) or Ctrl+J (Windows).
- On a meeting page, chat about that meeting.

## Ask better questions

- Name the account, deal, meeting, report, document or date range that you want Ergo to use.
- Ask for the output that you need. For example: next steps, risks, call prep, a follow-up draft or a short summary.
- Tell Ergo what to leave out when old or unrelated context makes the answer worse.
- Check the sources and the draft or CRM cards before you act on an answer.

## Get support

1. Click **Support** at the bottom of the left navigation. If you are in **Settings**, click **Back to Dashboard** first.
2. Write your question in the chat window. Ergo support answers there.

## What to include

- The affected account, person, integration, meeting, draft or report, and the approximate time.
- The prompt that you used and the result that you expected.
- The checks that you already tried.
- Screenshots that do not show sensitive data.

## Common issues

- You cannot find **Support**: it does not show in **Settings** or in the phone app. Go back to the **Dashboard**.
- You cannot find an account, meeting, draft or CRM record.
- A source is disconnected, out of date or on the wrong record.
- You cannot open a page that you expected to use. Your seat or role may not give access.
