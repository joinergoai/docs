Every person in Ergo has one seat type. The seat decides which features the person can use. It does not decide whose meetings the person can see. See [Seats, roles and visibility](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6821509431).

## Who can use this

- An **Owner** can add a person with any seat and can change the seat of a person who is in Ergo already.
- A team **Admin** can add a person with any seat, but only into a team that the Admin administers. A team Admin cannot change a seat later.

## The three seats

- **Standard**: full access. Chat with Ergo, CRM updates, email drafts, tasks, automations, the notetaker and every integration.
- **Notetaker**: records and views meetings. No CRM writes, no email drafts and no third-party note takers.
- **Spectator**: view-only. Ergo records nothing and writes nothing for the person.

| Feature | Standard | Notetaker | Spectator |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard**, **Meetings**, **Deals**, and **Settings → User integrations** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Chat about one meeting from its page | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Ergo Notetaker bot | Yes | Yes | No |
| Ergo Desktop Notetaker (only when your organization has the desktop app) | Yes | Yes | No |
| Own email and calendar connection | Yes | Yes | No |
| Own Slack account for pre-call DMs | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Chat with Ergo** | Yes | No | No |
| **Automations**, **Drafts**, **Tasks** (when your organization has Tasks), **Settings → Email Drafting** and **Knowledge Base** | Yes | No | No |
| CRM updates from meetings and email | Yes | No | No |
| Third-party note takers | Yes | No | No |

Reporting does not depend on the seat. A Member needs Reporting access from an Owner.

## Steps

To add a person with a seat:

1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Click **Add Member**.
3. Select the **Seat**.

To change the seat of a person (Owner only):

1. Open **Settings → Members** and click the person.
2. Change **Seat type**.

To see your own seat, open **Settings → Profile**.

## What to expect

- Some pages do not show for a limited seat. Nothing is broken.
- **Settings → User integrations** shows only the sections that the seat can use. A Spectator seat sees only **MCP**. A Notetaker seat does not see Gong or the third-party note takers.
- On the **Dashboard**, a Spectator seat sees a **Spectator access** note in **Upcoming Meetings**.
- A Spectator seat has no setup wizard. The app opens at the first sign-in.
- In **Settings → Members**, the **Automations** row of the person reads "Not available on a Spectator seat." (or Notetaker).
- A downgrade disconnects integrations. A downgrade to Spectator also cancels scheduled recordings. An upgrade does not reconnect them. The person must connect them again.
- Seats are billable. Check your agreement before you change a seat.

## Common issues

- A Spectator cannot use chat, or meetings do not update the CRM: the seat works as designed. Change the seat to **Standard**.
- A Spectator needs more to review:
  - More meetings: an Owner or the team Admin clicks **Edit** next to **Meeting access** in **Settings → Members**.
  - Dashboards: an Owner clicks **Grant** next to **Reporting access**. A Spectator seat can get Reporting access.
  - One recording: share the meeting.
- The **Seat type** control is off for you: only an Owner can change a seat.
