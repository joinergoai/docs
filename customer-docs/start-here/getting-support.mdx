Use the **Support** button in Ergo to send a message to the Ergo team.

## Who can use this

- Everyone who can sign in to Ergo.

## Steps

1. Sign in at dashboard.joinergo.com.
2. Click **Support** at the bottom of the left navigation. A chat window opens.
3. Type your question. Include the information below.
4. Send the message. The Ergo team answers in the same window.

## What to include

- What you tried to do, and what happened.
- The page where it happened, for example **Settings → CRM Properties** or a meeting page.
- The meeting title and date, or the deal name, if the problem is about one record.
- The exact error text on the screen, or a screenshot.
- Do not send passwords, API keys or private customer content.

## Before you contact support

- **A step is missing or a button is off:** your role or seat can be the cause. See [Seats, roles and visibility](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6821509431).
- **No new deals, or a field does not update:** see [CRM sync issues](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6503095041).
- **The bot did not join:** see [Notetaker did not join](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2299804002).

## What needs Ergo support

- Turning on a feature that is not on for your organization.
- Changing the main owner of the organization.
- Re-activating a person who was removed from the organization.
