You can add and edit deals in Ergo. Ergo writes each change to your CRM.

## Who can use this

- You need a **Standard** seat to add, edit, move or delete deals.
- **Notetaker** and **Spectator** seats can only read the Deals pages.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. If the page shows **No CRM Connected**, ask an owner or team admin to connect it in **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**.

## Steps

1. Open **Deals** and click **Add Deal** at the top right.
2. Fill in **Deal Name** (required). Check **Pipeline**, **Stage** and **Deal Owner**.
3. Optional: fill in **Amount**, **Close Date**, **Company** and **Contact**. Search for a record, or click **+ Create Company** or **+ Create Contact**.
4. Click **Create Deal**. Ergo makes the deal in your CRM and opens the deal page.
5. Fill in custom fields on the deal page.
6. To edit a deal, open it. Click a field in the left panel (deal name, amount, stage, pipeline, close date, owner) or in **Deal Info**.
7. To add an email, a meeting or a note to the deal, click **Log Activity**.

## What to expect

- The **Add Deal** dialog has one contact and no custom fields.
- Each edit writes to your CRM at once. There is no save step.
- In **List** view, you can click a cell to edit it. In **Board** view, you can edit the amount on the card.
- To see or restore an earlier value, click **Property history** next to a field.
- If a stage has required fields, Ergo opens a form when you move a deal to that stage. Fill in the fields, then confirm the move.

## Common issues

- **Add Deal** does not show: you do not have a **Standard** seat, or you are on the **Tasks** tab.
- A change does not save: check the property permissions in **Settings → CRM Settings → Permissions**.
- The same account shows under two names: see [How Ergo names and matches CRM records](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/5841172398).
