After a meeting with a prospect, Ergo can create a new deal in your CRM. It does this only when no existing deal matches and your settings allow it.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins** change these settings.

## When Ergo creates a deal

Ergo creates a deal when all of these are true:

- The meeting has external attendees, and Ergo finds no existing deal for their company. See [How Ergo names and matches CRM records](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/5841172398).
- **Allow creation** is on for **Deal** in **Settings → CRM Settings → Permissions**. It is on by default.
- The pipeline has an **Entry Point** stage. Ergo puts the new deal in that stage.
- If your organization has **Deal Qualification**, the conversation meets your criteria.

## Stop or reduce new deals

1. **Stop all new deals for a team:** open **Settings → CRM Settings**, select the team at the top right, and on the **Permissions** tab turn off **Allow creation** for **Deal**.
2. **Stop new deals for one person:** on the same tab, click **Add user override**, select the person, and turn off **Allow creation** for that person.
3. **Create fewer deals:** open **Settings → Advanced → Deal Qualification**. Raise **Confidence required**, or add a criterion. If you do not see this tab, ask Ergo support.

## What to expect

- Changes save at once. They apply to the next meetings, not to deals that Ergo already created.
- Ergo can still update existing deals when **Allow creation** is off. To stop updates, turn off **Allow updates**.
- To remove a deal that Ergo created by mistake, delete it or close it in your CRM.

## Common issues

- **Ergo made a second deal for a customer:** Ergo did not match the existing deal. Check the contact and company associations in your CRM, and the domains in **Settings → Advanced → Domains**.
- **Ergo made a deal for a partner:** add the partner's domain to **Partnership Domains**.
