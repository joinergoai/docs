Use this page when a deal, meeting or draft shows under the wrong account.

## Who can use this

- Everyone can read this page.
- An owner or team admin changes the settings that are named here.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. See [Connect your CRM](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2772377599).

## Steps

1. Compare the email addresses of the external attendees with the contacts in your CRM.
2. Make sure that your CRM associates each contact with the right company and deal.
3. Ask an owner or team admin to check **Settings → Advanced → Domains** (**Alternate Domains** and **Partnership Domains**).
4. Ask them to check **Settings → Company Details**.
5. Check your own **Alternate emails** in **Settings → Profile**.
6. Look in your CRM for duplicate companies or deals.

## What to expect

- For a meeting, Ergo finds the contact for each external email address. Then it uses the companies and deals that your CRM associates with that contact.
- Ergo also searches the CRM by the email domain that most external attendees have.
- **Alternate Domains** are other email domains of your own company. Ergo treats people on these domains as internal.
- **Partnership Domains** are the domains of your partners. Ergo does not use a partner attendee to find the customer company or deal.
- Ergo does not update the CRM from a meeting in which all external attendees are partners.
- Each domain list can hold a maximum of 50 domains.
- By default, the name of a new deal is the company name of the prospect. To change this rule, an owner or team admin edits the description of **Deal Name** in **Settings → CRM Properties**.

## Common issues

- A partner or reseller is treated like the customer: add the domain to **Partnership Domains**.
- A colleague on a second company domain counts as external: add the domain to **Alternate Domains**.
- The deal is not associated with the right contact: fix the association in your CRM first.
- The team limits Ergo to selected pipelines: Ergo does not use deals in other pipelines.
