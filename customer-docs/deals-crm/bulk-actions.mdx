Select deals to change many deals at once. Most actions start as soon as you select a value.

## Who can use this

- You need a **Standard** seat to select deals.
- **Notetaker** and **Spectator** seats can only read.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. If the page shows **No CRM Connected**, ask an owner or team admin to connect it in **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**.

## Steps

1. In **Board**, select deals with the checkbox on the card, or Shift+click.
2. In **List**, select deals with the checkbox in the row. There is no select-all checkbox.
3. Check the count (**N deals selected**) in the bar.
4. Select an action: **Move to stage**, **Set status**, **Assign owner**, **Mark as VIP**, **Generate Drafts** or **Delete**.
5. Press Esc or click **x** in the bar to clear the selection.

## What to expect

- **Move to stage**, **Set status**, **Assign owner** and **Mark as VIP** start at once. There is no confirmation step.
- Ergo writes the stage or the owner to your CRM for each deal.
- **Set status** has **Needs Attention**, **Reviewed** and **Clear Status**.
- If the target stage has required fields, Ergo does not move the deals in bulk. Move those deals one at a time.
- If some deals fail, Ergo shows "N of M deals failed to move".
- **Delete** permanently deletes the deals from your CRM. You cannot undo this. Ergo asks you to confirm first.
- You cannot change the close date of more than one deal at a time.
- The bar does not show on the **Tasks** tab.

## Common issues

- The selection has deals from the wrong filter: check the filter chips before you select.
- The target stage is not in the selected pipeline.
- Your CRM permissions prevent the update.
