The **Deals** page has built-in tabs. You can also make custom views for filters that you use often.

## Who can use this

- Everyone who can open **Deals**. A custom view is only for you. Your teammates do not see it.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. See [Connect your CRM](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2772377599).

## Steps

1. Open **Deals** and set the filters that you want.
2. Click **+** (**Add view**) after the tabs.
3. Select **Board** or **List**.
4. Type a name and press Enter.
5. To limit the view to one pipeline, click the gear icon (**Board settings**) → **Custom Views**. Select **All Pipelines** or **Specific Pipeline** for the view.
6. To show or hide **This Week** or **Follow Ups**, click the gear icon → **Views**. Use the switch on the tab card under **Built-in View Defaults**.
7. To delete a view, click **x** on its tab.

## What to expect

- The built-in tabs are **Deals** (your deals) and **High Priority** (your top 20% of deals by priority score, and your VIP deals). **High Priority** was called **Ergo View**.
- The other two built-in tabs are **This Week** and **Follow Ups**. Some workspaces also have a **Tasks** tab.
- **Follow Ups** shows the result of **Review & Draft Follow-Ups**. It is not a filter view.
- A new custom view starts with the filters on the screen. Later changes save automatically.
- **x** deletes the view at once, with no confirmation.
- On **Built-in View Defaults** you can change the default filters of each built-in tab. **Reset to defaults** puts them back. These changes are also only for you.

## Common issues

- A view shows no deals: it has old filters. Change the filters in the view.
- The view is missing: it is set to **Specific Pipeline** and a different pipeline is selected.
- A built-in tab uses **List** when you expect **Board**: Ergo remembers your last choice for each tab.
