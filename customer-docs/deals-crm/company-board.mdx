Select **Companies** on the **Deals** page to see account-level context. It uses the same selector as the pipelines.

## Who can use this

- You need a **Standard** seat to move companies.
- **Notetaker** and **Spectator** seats can only read.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. If the page shows **No CRM Connected**, ask an owner or team admin to connect it in **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**.

## Steps

1. Open **Deals**. Use the selector next to the **Board** / **List** switch to select **Companies**.
2. Review the companies in **List**.
3. To use **Board**, click the gear icon (**Board settings**) → **General** → **Grouping property**. Select a single-select or dropdown company property.
4. In **Board**, drag a company card to a different column.
5. To move more than one company, select the companies and use **Move to column**.
6. Click a company to open it.

## What to expect

- **Board** is off until you set a grouping property. Only **List** works before that.
- The values of the grouping property become the columns.
- When you move a company, Ergo changes the grouping property of that company in your CRM.
- Use **Search companies...** to search.
- Click **+ Filter** to filter by **Owner**, **Health**, **Deal Count**, **Total Value**, **Latest Meeting Outcome**, **Domain** or **Company Name**.
- In **Board**, the sort menu has **Company Name**, **Domain**, **Deal Count**, **Total Value**, **Last Correspondence** and **Health**. In **List**, click a column header to sort.
- To open **Companies** first each time, click the gear icon → **General** → **Default view** → **Companies**.

## Common issues

- **Board** is disabled: set a grouping property.
- You see deals, not companies: you are on a pipeline. Select **Companies**.
- A company field is empty: map the company property. See [CRM properties setup](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3371162277).
