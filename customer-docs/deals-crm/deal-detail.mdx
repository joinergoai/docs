Open a deal to see its fields, activity, emails and meetings in one place.

## Who can use this

- Everyone who can open **Deals**.
- You need a **Standard** seat to edit a deal and to use **Draft Email**. **Notetaker** and **Spectator** seats can only read.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. If the page shows **No CRM Connected**, ask an owner or team admin to connect it in **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**.

## Steps

1. Open a deal from **Board** or **List** view.
2. Use the tabs **Activity**, **Deal Info**, **Emails** and **Meetings**.
3. On **Activity**, read the **AI Overview**, the **Deal Health** card and the **Activity Timeline**.
4. Check the recent emails and meetings. Then click **Draft Email** at the right of the tab row.
5. To add an email, a meeting or a note, click **Log Activity**.
6. To make a tab with your own fields, click **+** (**Add tab**). Type a name and click **Create Tab**.
7. Click **Back** or press Esc to go back to the board.

## What to expect

- The left panel shows the deal fields. A change to a field writes to your CRM.
- **Draft Email** opens the chat panel. Ergo drafts the next follow-up email for the deal.
- The right panel shows the **Company** card, the **Contacts** list, the linked Slack channels and the **Documents** of the deal.
- Some workspaces also have the tabs **Stakeholders** and **Tasks**.
- Ergo saves a custom tab in this browser only. Your teammates and your other browsers do not show it.
- **CRM updates to review** shows at the top of **Activity** only when updates wait for approval.

## Common issues

- Activity is missing: it may belong to the company or a contact, not the deal.
- A field is missing: a custom tab or the field list hides it.
- The deal is not found: it may no longer exist in your CRM.
