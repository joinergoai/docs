Select deals and let Ergo make drafts for all of them. Ergo does not send the drafts.

## Who can use this

- You need a **Standard** seat.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM and your email. See [Connect email and calendar](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4928954637).

## Steps

1. On **Deals**, select the deals that need a follow-up. Select a maximum of 30 deals.
2. Click **Generate Drafts** for email drafts.
3. For Slack drafts, click the arrow next to the button. Select **Slack Draft**.
4. Add an optional instruction. It applies to all the selected deals.
5. Wait until the chip on each deal changes from **Queued** and **Drafting** to **Draft**.
6. Click **Draft** to open the draft. Check the recipient, the thread and the text, then send.
7. In **List** view, select 2 or more deals that have a draft. Click **Go Mode** to review the drafts one after the other.

## What to expect

- You can start a maximum of 3 requests in one minute.
- In **List** view, the chip is in the **Draft** column.
- **Re-draft** shows when new correspondence arrived after the draft.
- If Ergo skips a deal, a message shows the deal name and the reason.
- To remove the drafts of the selected deals, select **Clear Drafts** in the arrow menu.
- Draft quality depends on the meetings, emails and CRM fields linked to each deal.

## Common issues

- A deal has no contact email: Ergo can skip it.
- The deals need different instructions: use a smaller selection.
- Recent context is on a different record.
