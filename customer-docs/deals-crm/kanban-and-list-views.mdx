Use **Board** to scan or move deals by stage. Use **List** to compare deals in a table.

## Who can use this

- You need a **Standard** seat to move deals.
- **Notetaker** and **Spectator** seats can only read the Deals pages.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. If the page shows **No CRM Connected**, ask an owner or team admin to connect it in **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**.

## Steps

1. Open **Deals** and select a pipeline.
2. Select **Board** to see stage columns. Select **List** to see a table.
3. In **Board**, drag a deal card to a different stage column to move the deal. If the stage has required fields, Ergo shows a form first.
4. To change the fields on a deal card, click the gear icon (**Board settings**) → **Card Fields**. Select the fields.
5. To change the columns in **List**, click **Columns** and select the fields under **Standard Fields** and **Custom Fields**.
6. Click a column header to sort the list.

## What to expect

- Ergo writes the new stage to your CRM when you move a deal.
- A card shows a maximum of 3 fields. The amount and the health score always show. The switch **Expanded card text** wraps long text.
- **Card Fields** applies to all tabs in the selected pipeline.
- **Columns** applies to the current tab in the selected pipeline.
- A stage column with no deals collapses. Click a column header to collapse or expand a column.
- The **Follow Ups** tab shows a list only. It has no **Columns** button.
- List columns can show standard fields and your mapped custom fields.

## Common issues

- A stage column is missing: a filter hides its deals. To show only some stages, click **+ Filter** → **Stage** or **Stage Status**. A filter does not change the records.
- A custom field is not a column choice: map the property first. See [CRM properties setup](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3371162277).
