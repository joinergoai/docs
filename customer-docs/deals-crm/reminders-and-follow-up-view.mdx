The **Follow Ups** tab shows the deals that Ergo selected in your last pipeline review. Ergo drafts an email for each deal.

## Who can use this

- You need a **Standard** seat to run the review.
- **Notetaker** and **Spectator** seats can only read.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. If the page shows **No CRM Connected**, ask an owner or team admin to connect it in **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**.

## Steps

1. Open **Deals** and select the pipeline.
2. Click **Review & Draft Follow-Ups** at the top right.
3. Wait one or two minutes. You can continue to work. Ergo reads your open deals (a maximum of 60) and fills the **Follow Ups** tab.
4. On **Follow Ups**, read the **Why** column for each deal. Click **Draft** to open the email draft.
5. Click **Not needed** to remove a deal from the list.
6. To review the deals of other owners, change the **Owner** filter on **Follow Ups**. Then click **Re-run Review**.
7. If your workspace has the **Tasks** tab, use it to see your open tasks. To add a task, open a deal, click the bell icon next to the deal name, then click **Add Task**.

## What to expect

- **Follow Ups** is not a filter. It shows the result of the review. Before the first review, it shows **Review your pipeline**.
- The tab is a list. Only the **Owner** filter works on it.
- A review takes a minute or two. Only one review can run at a time for each pipeline. After a review, wait 5 minutes.
- **Re-run Review** replaces the list.
- The number on **Follow Ups** is the number of deals from your last review. The number on **Tasks** is the number of open tasks.
- The reminders tab is removed. Reminders are now tasks, and only some workspaces have them.

## Common issues

- **Follow Ups** is empty: run **Review & Draft Follow-Ups**.
- A deal is missing from the list: check the **Owner** filter, then run **Re-run Review**.
- There is no **Tasks** tab: your workspace does not have tasks.
