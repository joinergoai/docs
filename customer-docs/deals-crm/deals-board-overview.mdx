**Deals** shows the deals from your CRM. Use it to review your pipeline and to follow up.

## Who can use this

- You need a **Standard** seat to add, edit, move, select or delete deals.
- **Notetaker** and **Spectator** seats can only read the Deals pages.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. If the page shows **No CRM Connected**, ask an owner or team admin to connect it in **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**. See [Connect your CRM](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2772377599).

## Steps

1. Open **Deals** from the left navigation.
2. Use the selector next to the **Board** / **List** switch to select a CRM pipeline or **Companies**.
3. Select a tab: **Deals**, **High Priority**, **This Week** or **Follow Ups**.
4. Select **Board** to see stage columns. Select **List** to see a table.
5. Click **+ Filter** to add a filter. Type 3 characters or more in **Search deals...** to search.
6. Click **Review & Draft Follow-Ups**. Ergo finds the deals that need a reply and drafts the emails.
7. Click **Add Deal** to make a deal in your CRM.
8. Click the gear icon (**Board settings**). Set the default pipeline, the default filters of the built-in tabs and the fields on the deal cards.

## What to expect

- **Deals** shows your deals. **High Priority** shows your top 20% of deals by priority score, and your VIP deals.
- **This Week** shows your deals with a meeting this week. **Follow Ups** shows the deals that Ergo found in your last pipeline review.
- Some workspaces also have a **Tasks** tab.
- To set which pipeline opens first, click the gear icon → **Views** → **Default pipeline**.
- A link to **Deals** keeps the pipeline, the tab and the **Board** or **List** mode. It does not keep filters, search or sort.
- The **Follow Ups** tab shows a list only.

## Common issues

- A deal is not on the board: look at the **Owner** chip first. Each built-in tab shows only your deals. Change or remove the **Owner** chip to see other owners.
- Then clear the search box and the other filter chips.
- The wrong pipeline, or **Companies**, is selected.
