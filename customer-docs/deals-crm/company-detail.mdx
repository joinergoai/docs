Open a company to see account context across deals and communications.

## Who can use this

- Everyone who can open **Deals**. **Notetaker** and **Spectator** seats can only read.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. If the page shows **No CRM Connected**, ask an owner or team admin to connect it in **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**.

## Steps

1. On **Deals**, select **Companies**. Open a company.
2. Use the tabs **Activity**, **Company Info**, **Emails** and **Meetings**.
3. On **Activity**, read the **AI Account Overview**, the **Account Health** card, the deals of the company and the **Activity Timeline**.
4. Check the **Contacts** list in the right panel.
5. Check the company fields in the left panel.

## What to expect

- Company detail depends on the company records and associations in your CRM.
- Company activity can include emails, meetings, Slack activity and related deals, when they are connected and associated.
- Ergo scores account health only for a company that has a closed-won deal. Other companies show **Not scored yet** or no health card.
- The right panel also shows the linked Slack channels.
- Some workspaces also have the tabs **Stakeholders** and **Tasks**.
- **Log Activity** is in the **Activity Timeline**.

## Common issues

- The company is not associated with the deal: fix the association in your CRM.
- The company domains are incomplete or duplicated in the CRM.
- The company has no activity yet.
