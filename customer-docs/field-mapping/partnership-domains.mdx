Add the email domains of your partners, resellers and agencies. Ergo then does not use them to find the customer of a meeting.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- A **Member** does not see the page **Advanced**.

## Before you start

- Decide if a domain is a domain of your own organization or of a partner.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Advanced → Domains**.
2. Below **Partnership Domains**, type the domain of a partner and press Enter.
3. To remove a domain, click the **×** on the domain.
4. Do not add a customer domain to this list.
5. Do not add a domain of your own organization to this list. Use **Alternate Domains** for it.
6. Process one meeting that has a partner and a customer. Check that Ergo selects the customer company.

## What to expect

- Ergo saves a domain when you press Enter. There is no **Save** button.
- You can add 50 domains at most.
- There are two lists only: **Alternate Domains** and **Partnership Domains**.
- Ergo does not create or update a company for a partner domain.
- Ergo does not use partner people to find the customer of a meeting.
- Ergo can still create or update the contact of a partner attendee and link the contact to the deal.
- Ergo treats partnership domains as internal for drafting.

## Common issues

- A partner domain is also used by a customer: do not add it.
- A domain is in the wrong list: remove it and add it to the right list.
- Ergo picks the wrong company for a meeting: see [How Ergo names and matches CRM records](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/5841172398).
