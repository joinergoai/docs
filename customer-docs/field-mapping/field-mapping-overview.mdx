Set up Ergo for your CRM before Ergo updates CRM records. The setup is on five pages in **Settings**.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- A team Admin sees only the teams that the Admin manages. A **Member** does not see these pages.
- **CRM Properties**, **CRM Stages** and **CRM Settings** are for one team. Select the team at the top right of the page.

## Before you start

- Open **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**. Make sure that your CRM is connected. See [Connect your CRM](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2772377599).

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → CRM Properties**. Select the team. Click **Sync From CRM**. Select the deal, contact and company fields. Click **Sync Selected Properties**.
2. Open each field. Write a **Description** and set the **Write behavior**.
3. Open **Settings → CRM Stages**. Click **Sync From CRM**, select the stages and click **Sync Stages**.
4. Click **Stage Settings**. Turn on **Entry Point** for the stage where a new deal starts. Click **Save Changes**.
5. Open **Settings → CRM Settings**. Check the tabs **Permissions**, **CRM behavior** and **Meeting titles**.
6. Open **Settings → Company Details** and **Settings → Advanced**.
7. Test one meeting before you turn on more automations.

## What to expect

- **CRM Properties**, **CRM Stages** and **CRM Settings** are in the group **Team**. Do the setup again for each team.
- **Company Details** describes how you sell. Ergo uses it in drafts, summaries and CRM updates.
- **CRM Settings → Meeting titles** sets which meetings count as sales meetings.
- **CRM Settings → CRM behavior** sets CRM logging and update sensitivity.
- **Advanced → Domains** holds your alternate domains and your partner domains.
- With HubSpot or Attio, the tab **Edit** in the stage sync window changes the stages in the CRM.

## Common issues

- A CRM page shows "Connect your CRM to continue": connect the CRM first.
- A team has no fields: sync the fields for that team.
- A field or stage changed in the CRM: sync again. See [Stage drift conflicts](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1509454679).
