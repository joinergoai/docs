CRM properties are the deal, contact and company fields that Ergo can read and write. Each team has its own list.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- A team Admin sees only the teams that the Admin manages. A **Member** does not see this page.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM. See [Connect your CRM](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2772377599).
- Get the approval of your CRM owner before you push a new field to the CRM.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → CRM Properties**. Select the team in the team selector at the top right.
2. Click **Sync From CRM**. The window **Select Properties to Sync from CRM** opens.
3. Use the tabs **Deal**, **Contact** and **Company**. **Custom** shows the fields that your company made. **CRM Defined** shows the standard fields of your CRM.
4. Click each field that Ergo must use. Keep **Auto-generate descriptions** selected. Click **Sync Selected Properties**.
5. Click a field to open it. Check the **Description**, the **Field type** and the **Options**.
6. Set the **Write behavior**. Select **Require approval before writing** if a person must approve each value.
7. To make a field that is not in your CRM, click **Add Property**. Fill in **Property Name**, **Field Type** and **Description**. Click **Create Property**. Then click **Push to CRM** on the field row.
8. To remove a field from the team, click the trash icon (**Remove field**) on the field row.

## What to expect

- **Write behavior** has three values: **Ergo can always write to this field**, **Ergo will only write if not already populated** and **Ergo will never write to this field**.
- Changes to a field save automatically.
- Fields that are already mapped show **(synced)**. You cannot clear them in the sync window.
- After the sync, the message **Added N properties** shows. Ergo then writes a first description for each new field.
- **Add Property** makes the field in Ergo only. **Push to CRM** needs a description.
- **Add Default** adds the fields that Ergo recommends.
- A change to a field name, type or option applies to all teams. Descriptions and write rules are for one team.
- A field with no description shows **Incomplete**. Click **Ask Ergo** for a draft, then correct it.
- **Remove field** removes the field at once, with no confirmation.

## Common issues

- A field changed in the CRM: the row shows **Resolve Conflict**. Click it. Select **Keep CRM Version** or **Update CRM**.
- A standard CRM field is missing in the sync window: change the view from **Custom** to **CRM Defined**.
- A field shows in Ergo but Ergo cannot write to it: check **Write behavior** and the permission of the CRM user.
