Deal Qualification is a check for new deals. You write the criteria. Before Ergo creates a deal from a meeting, it checks that the conversation has enough evidence for each criterion. If not, Ergo does not create the deal.

This feature is not on for all organizations. If you do not see the tab, contact Ergo support.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**. A **Member** does not see **Settings → Advanced**.

## Before you start

- Decide what a real opportunity must show: for example budget, authority, need and timeline.
- Deal Qualification applies to new deals only. To set what a deal needs before it moves to a stage, use **Entry Criteria** on the stage in **Settings → CRM Stages**.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Advanced → Deal Qualification**. If you do not see this tab, the feature is not on for your organization.
2. Click **Create your own**, or select the preset **BANT**, **MEDDIC** or **SPICED**.
3. Click **Add Criterion**. Type a **Criterion name**.
4. In **What should Ergo look for?**, write the evidence that Ergo must find in the conversation.
5. Select the **Confidence required**: **Low**, **Medium** or **High**. Click **Save Criterion**.
6. Make sure that the switch **Enabled** is on.
7. Process one meeting with a new prospect. Make sure that Ergo creates the deal only when the criteria have evidence.

## What to expect

- You can have 12 criteria at most.
- The result shows on the deal page in Ergo, in the cards **Deal Creation Qualification** and **Deal Stage Qualification**.
- Ergo does not write the qualification result to a CRM field.

## Common issues

- No tab **Deal Qualification**: the feature is not on for your organization.
- Too few deals are created: lower the **Confidence required**, or make **What should Ergo look for?** wider.
- Too many deals are created: raise the **Confidence required**, or add a criterion.
