You can fill a newly mapped CRM field from past meetings, emails and calls. You cannot start this backfill in the app. Ergo support runs it for you.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** or a team **Admin** asks Ergo support for the backfill.

## Before you start

- Map the field first. Open **Settings → CRM Properties**. Sync the field and write its **Description**. Make sure that its **Write behavior** lets Ergo write.
- Ergo can backfill only custom fields that are mapped and that Ergo can write. Deal name, amount and close date are not possible.

## Steps

1. If past meetings are not in Ergo, import them. Open **Settings → Org integrations → Meeting backfill**. Or open **Settings → User integrations → Meeting backfill**.
2. Connect the tool that has your past meetings.
3. Send Ergo support the list of fields, the record type (deal, contact or company) and the scope. For example, open deals only, or one pipeline.
4. Ask for a test run on a small sample. Review the proposed values.
5. Tell Ergo support to start the full run.
6. Check some records in your CRM after the run.

## What to expect

- Ergo backfills a record only if a meeting, an email thread or a call is linked to that record in Ergo.
- Ergo fills only empty fields. It does not replace a value.
- A test run covers 25 records at most.
- The full run writes directly to your CRM. A field that needs approval is also written directly.
- One run takes 5,000 records at most, and 1 to 20 fields of one record type.
- A new field does not fill itself for existing deals.

## Common issues

- The field was mapped after the meetings were processed: ask for a backfill.
- Past meetings are missing or linked to the wrong record: import them and check the links.
- The field is not possible: its **Write behavior** is **Ergo will never write to this field**, or the field is read-only in the CRM.
