Alternate domains and alternate emails tell Ergo who belongs to **your own** organization. Ergo treats people on these domains as colleagues, not as customers.

## Who can use this

- **Alternate Domains:** the organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- **Alternate emails:** each person, for their own profile.

## Before you start

- Add only domains that your organization owns. Do not add the domain of a customer or of a partner.
- If you add a customer domain, Ergo treats the people of that customer as internal. Ergo then does not find the customer company or the deal for their meetings.
- For partner or client domains that you work with, use **Partnership Domains**.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Advanced → Domains**.
2. Below **Alternate Domains**, type a domain that your organization owns and press Enter.
3. To remove a domain, click the **×** on the domain.
4. Each person: open **Settings → Profile** and click the pencil next to **Alternate emails**.
5. Type a second work address, click **Add**, then **Save changes**.
6. Process one meeting. Make sure that Ergo shows your colleagues as internal and the customer as external.

## What to expect

- You can add 50 domains at most.
- Each person can add 3 alternate emails at most. Personal email domains are refused.
- Ergo looks for the customer company only among the external attendees of a meeting.

## Common issues

- A customer shows as internal: their domain is in **Alternate Domains**. Remove it.
- A colleague shows as external: add their domain to **Alternate Domains**, or ask them to add the address to **Alternate emails**.
