**Restrict to pipeline** is one switch. When it is on, Ergo looks up deals only in the pipeline of your team.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- A team Admin sees only the teams that the Admin manages. A **Member** does not see this page.

## Before you start

- Sync the stages of one pipeline for the team. See [Pipeline stages](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1501601101).

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → CRM Stages**. Select the team in the team selector at the top right.
2. Make sure that the team has stages from one pipeline only.
3. Click the **⋯** button to the right of **Sync From CRM**.
4. Turn on **Restrict to pipeline**.
5. Process one meeting. Check that Ergo updates a deal in the pipeline of the team, not a deal in a different pipeline.

## What to expect

- The switch shows only when the team has stages from exactly one pipeline.
- With the switch off, Ergo can find a deal in any pipeline of your CRM.
- The switch limits where Ergo looks for deals. It does not give or remove the right to write a stage.
- This switch does not choose the pipelines that a team uses. Use **Sync From CRM** for that.
- The switch does not change the Deals board.

## Common issues

- The switch does not show: the team has stages from two or more pipelines.
- Ergo updates a deal in a different pipeline: the switch is off.
- Ergo does not find a deal that exists: the deal is in a different pipeline and the switch is on.
