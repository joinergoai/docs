A connected CRM is not enough. Ergo also needs mapped fields, synced stages and write permissions.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- A team Admin sees only the teams that the Admin manages. A **Member** cannot do this setup.

## Before you start

- Use a CRM account that can read and update the fields that Ergo needs.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**. Make sure that your CRM is connected.
2. Open **Settings → CRM Properties**. Select the team at the top right.
3. Click **Sync From CRM**. In the tabs **Deal**, **Contact** and **Company**, select the fields. Click **Sync Selected Properties**.
4. Open each field. Write a **Description** and set the **Write behavior**.
5. Open **Settings → CRM Stages**. Click **Sync From CRM**, select your stages and click **Sync Stages**.
6. Click **Stage Settings**. In each pipeline, turn on **Entry Point** for the stage where a new deal starts. Click **Save Changes**.
7. Open **Settings → CRM Settings → Permissions**. Make sure that **Allow creation** and **Allow updates** are on for the records that Ergo must write.
8. Process one meeting. Check that Ergo writes the fields and the stage to one CRM record.

## What to expect

- Each team has its own field list.
- **Amount** and **Close Date** start as **Ergo will never write to this field**. Change them if Ergo must fill them.
- The record-owner field of your CRM is not in the sync window. Ergo sets the record owner itself.
- A field that is read-only in your CRM shows **Read only in CRM**. Ergo does not write to it.
- Ergo settings do not override the permissions of your CRM.
- Ergo cannot create a deal in a pipeline that has no entry point. The page shows **Pipeline entry point required**.

## Common issues

- The CRM user cannot write to the field: change the permission in your CRM.
- A field exists in the CRM but not in Ergo: sync it.
- A stage or pipeline changed in the CRM: sync again.
