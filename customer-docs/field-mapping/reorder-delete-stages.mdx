There are two ways to delete a stage. One changes Ergo only. The other changes your CRM.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- A team Admin sees only the teams that the Admin manages.

## Before you start

- With HubSpot or Attio, tell your CRM owner before you change stages in the CRM.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → CRM Stages**. Select the team and the **Pipeline**.
2. To remove a stage from the team in Ergo only, click the trash icon (**Delete stage**) on the stage row.
3. To change stages in the CRM (HubSpot or Attio), click **Sync From CRM**. Open the tab **Edit**.
4. To rename a stage, click its name, type the new name and press Enter.
5. To reorder stages (HubSpot), drag a stage to its new position. Click **Save Order**.
6. To delete a stage in the CRM, click the trash icon on the tab **Edit**. Click **Delete**.
7. Click **Close**. Check the stage list on the page.

## What to expect

- The trash icon on a stage row has no confirmation. It does not change your CRM.
- The tab **Edit** shows only for HubSpot and Attio. It warns that changes go directly to your CRM.
- A stage name on this tab must have 3 to 255 characters.
- The stage order comes from your CRM. The stage list has no reorder control.
- HubSpot does not delete a stage that has deals. Move the deals first.
- Attio moves the deals of a deleted stage to no stage. You cannot undo this.
- With Salesforce, change the stages in Salesforce.
- A stage that has a new name in the CRM shows **Resolve Conflict**. A stage that is no longer in the CRM shows **Not in CRM**.

## Common issues

- A CRM admin already changed the stage in the CRM: sync again and resolve the conflict.
- A new stage in the CRM is missing: select it in **Sync From CRM**.
- After a rename, check the stage descriptions.
