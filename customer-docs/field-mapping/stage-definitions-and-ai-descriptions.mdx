A stage description tells Ergo what a stage means. Ergo uses it when it decides if a deal belongs in a stage.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- The description is for the team that you selected.

## Before you start

- Sync your stages. See [Pipeline stages](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1501601101).

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → CRM Stages**. Select the team and the **Pipeline**.
2. Click a stage to open it.
3. Write the **Description**. Or click **Ask Ergo** for a draft, then correct it.
4. In **Entry Criteria**, list the evidence that Ergo must find before it selects the stage.
5. In **Required to enter this stage**, list the deal fields that a deal must have before Ergo moves it into the stage.
6. Repeat for each stage.

## What to expect

- A stage with no description shows the badge **Incomplete**.
- When you sync stages, **Auto-generate descriptions** is selected. Ergo writes a first description for each new stage. Read each one and correct it.
- The first stage of a pipeline has no **Entry Criteria**.
- **Required to enter this stage** can list a maximum of 20 deal fields, for all teams.
- The stage row shows the **Stage Name** and the **Internal ID** from your CRM.
- The **Description**, **Entry Criteria** and **Exit Points** apply to the selected team. A change to the **Stage Name** applies to all teams.
- Descriptions guide Ergo. They do not override your CRM rules.

## Common issues

- Two stages have similar names: write what is different in the evidence for each stage.
- A description describes the sales process in general: write the evidence for this stage only.
- Your CRM stages changed: check the descriptions again.

## Writing advice

- Keep descriptions short, specific and based on evidence.
- Add an exclusion when Ergo often reads a phrase wrongly.
