Default stages help a team start. A default stage is in Ergo only until you add it to your CRM.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- A team Admin sees only the teams that the Admin manages.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM and sync your stages. See [Pipeline stages](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1501601101).

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → CRM Stages**. Select the team and the **Pipeline**.
2. Click **Add Default**. The window **Add Default Pipeline Stages** opens.
3. Select the pipeline. Select the default stages that match your sales process.
4. Click the **Add … Stages** button.
5. Open each new stage. Change the **Stage Name** and the **Description** to match your process.
6. With HubSpot or Attio, click **Add stage to CRM** on each new stage, or click **Bulk Add to CRM**.
7. Click **Stage Settings**. Set the **Entry Point** and check the **Status** of each stage. Click **Save Changes**.

## What to expect

- The default stages are Lead, Demo & Qualification, Follow-Up & Negotiation, Contract Sent, Future Need, Closed Won, Closed Lost, Unqualified and Churned.
- Ergo never makes a stage two times.
- Each default stage has a description from Ergo.
- **Closed Won** gets the status **Won**. **Closed Lost**, **Churned** and **Unqualified** get **Lost**. The others get **Active**. **Entry Point** is off.
- You cannot set the stage order in the window.
- With HubSpot, Salesforce or Attio, a default stage shows **Not in CRM**. Ergo does not move deals into it.
- With Salesforce, make the stage in Salesforce. Then add it with **Sync From CRM**.
- A change to the **Stage Name** applies to all teams.

## Common issues

- The defaults do not match your process: add only the stages that match.
- The wrong pipeline is selected: select the right pipeline in the window before you add stages.
- A stage has no useful description: write one. See [Stage definitions and AI descriptions](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/7126058981).
