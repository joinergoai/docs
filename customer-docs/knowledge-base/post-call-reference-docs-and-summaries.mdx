Reference documents are files that Ergo uses when it drafts post-call emails. Ergo reads a short summary of each file.

## Who can use this

- The Owner and Admins manage the organization documents.
- The Admins of a team manage the documents of that team.
- Ergo uses team documents together with the organization documents for the members of that team.

## Before you start

- Choose a playbook or guide that helps drafts. Each file must be 25 MB or smaller.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Email Drafting** in the **Team** group.
2. For documents for all users, open the **Organization** tab. For documents for one team, open the **Team** tab and select the team.
3. In **Reference documents**, click **Click to upload or drag and drop**, or drag a file into the area.
4. Wait until **Generating summary…** goes away. Read the summary.
5. To change the summary, click **Edit**, change the text, then click **Save**. The maximum is 8,000 characters.
6. If you see **Summary failed**, click **Retry**.
7. To remove a document, click the trash icon on its row.

## What to expect

- You can keep a maximum of 20 reference documents for the organization and 20 for each team.
- At the limit, delete a document before you upload a new one.
- After an Admin changes a summary, the badge **Edited** shows.
- Ergo uses the summary as short drafting context. It reads the full content when needed.
- Reference documents guide drafts. They do not replace your review of each draft.

## Common issues

- The upload area does not accept a file: you are at the limit of 20.
- You cannot manage the documents of a team: only Admins of that team can.
- **Summary failed** shows: click **Retry**.
