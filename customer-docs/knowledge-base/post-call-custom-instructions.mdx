Post-call instructions tell Ergo how to write follow-up drafts after meetings. There are three layers: organization, team and personal.

## Who can use this

- The Owner and team Admins, for organization and team instructions. Only an Admin of a team can change the instructions of that team.
- Each person with a Standard seat, for personal instructions.

## Before you start

- Decide which rules go where. Put company rules in the organization layer. Put role or segment rules in the team layer. Put tone, sign-off and personal style in the personal layer.

## Steps

1. Owner or team Admin: open **Settings → Email Drafting** in the **Team** group.
2. For rules for all users, open the **Organization** tab. Type in **Organization post-call instructions**.
3. For rules for one team, open the **Team** tab. Select the team. Type in **Post-call instructions**.
4. Wait for **Saved**. Ergo saves the text automatically. The maximum is 8,000 characters.
5. To add a playbook, add a file in **Reference documents** on the same tab.
6. Each user: open **Settings → Email Drafting** in the **Your settings** group, then the **Personalization** tab.
7. Type in **My post-call instructions**. Ergo saves the text automatically.

## What to expect

- Ergo adds organization instructions to post-call drafts for all users.
- Ergo adds team instructions only to the drafts of the members of that team.
- Personal instructions apply only to your own post-call drafts.
- Members do not see the organization or team instructions. Ergo adds them to each draft automatically.
- Changes affect future drafts. They do not change drafts that exist.

## Common issues

- You do not see the **Team** page: only the Owner and team Admins see it.
- You do not see **Email Drafting** in **Your settings**: you need a Standard seat.
- A draft ignores a rule: make the instruction more specific, and keep it current.
