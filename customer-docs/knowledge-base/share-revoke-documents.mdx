Use **Share** to let other people open an artifact. To stop sharing, change the option back to **Private** or **Organization**.

## Who can use this

- The creator of an artifact. Only the creator sees **Share**.

## Before you start

- Find the artifact. Open **Search** (⌘K), type **Artifacts** and press Enter.
- Decide who needs the file: users in your organization, or people outside.

## Steps

1. On the artifact, open the **⋯** menu and click **Share**.
2. In the **Share document** dialog, select **Public link** (for people outside Ergo) or **Organization** (for users in your organization).
3. Click **Copy** and send the link only to the correct people.
4. To stop the link, open **Share** again and select **Private** or **Organization**. The public link stops immediately.

## What to expect

- A person who opens a public link must type an email address and then a 6-digit code from an email. Then the file shows.
- A person with the **Public link** can view and download the file.
- A person with the **Org link** must sign in to Ergo first.
- There is no **Revoke** button. Selecting **Private** or **Organization** removes the public link.
- Stopping a link does not delete the artifact.
- If the artifact is linked to a deal, you cannot select **Private**. Unlink the artifact from its deals first.

## Common issues

- You do not see **Share**: you are not the creator of the artifact.
- **Private** is not available: the artifact is linked to a deal.
- An outside reader cannot open the link: they must confirm an email address with a code.
