Connect Outreach so that Ergo can import your recorded calls. One connection serves the whole organization.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.

## Before you start

- Make sure that call recording is on in Outreach.
- Use an Outreach account that can approve access to calls, prospects, users and webhooks.
- Make sure that the Outreach user and the prospect each have an email address in Outreach.

## Steps

1. Sign in to Ergo as the Owner or a team Admin.
2. Open **Settings → Org integrations**. In **Dialers**, find **Outreach** and select **Connect**.
3. Sign in to Outreach and approve the access.
4. Back in Ergo, make sure that the **Outreach** row shows the label **Connected** and the button **Disconnect**.
5. Make one recorded call of 60 seconds or longer in Outreach. Then look for the call in **Meetings**.

## What to expect

- Ergo makes the Outreach webhook itself when you connect. You do not set up call events.
- Ergo imports an Outreach call only when the call is completed, has a recording, and is 60 seconds or longer.
- This connection is for Outreach dialer calls. It does not read emails that Outreach sequences send. To give Ergo email context, connect Google Workspace or Microsoft 365.

## Common issues

- A call does not appear: the call was shorter than 60 seconds, had no recording, or was not completed.
- A call does not appear: the Outreach user or the prospect has no email address in Outreach.
- You do not see **Connect**: only the Owner or a team Admin can connect a dialer.
