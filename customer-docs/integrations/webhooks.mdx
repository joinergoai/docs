A webhook sends each finished meeting to your own system. Ergo sends the event `notetaker.meeting.completed` when a meeting finishes processing.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- Webhooks are not available in organizations with HIPAA mode.

## Before you start

- Have the URL of your endpoint ready (for example a Zapier or n8n webhook URL).

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Webhooks**.
2. Click **Add webhook**.
3. Type a name and the URL of your endpoint.
4. Select whose meetings the webhook sends.
5. If necessary, add title phrases, so that only meetings with these words in the title are sent.
6. Make sure that **Send meetings to this webhook** is on. Save the webhook.
7. Copy the signing secret and use it in your system to check that each event comes from Ergo.

## What to expect

- Each organization can have 10 webhooks.
- Webhooks are shared across the organization. Each one sends meetings only for the people that you select.
- The form shows an example of the data that Ergo sends.

## Common issues

- No **Webhooks** page: you are a Member, or your organization has HIPAA mode.
- No events arrive: make sure that the switch is on, that the meeting is from a person that you selected, and that the title phrases match.
