Slack has two settings. **Slack monitoring** is for the whole organization. **Pre-call context DM** is for each person.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins** turn on **Slack monitoring**.
- Each person turns on their own **Pre-call context DM**.

## Before you start

- Connect Slack first. See [Slack](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/7075762783).
- Connect your own Slack account in **Settings → User integrations → Slack**. **Pick channels** is off until you do.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Org integrations**. In the **Workspaces** section, click the gear in the **Slack** row. The **Slack settings** window opens.
2. Turn on **Slack monitoring**.
3. At **Channels Ergo watches**, click **Pick channels**. Select the channels and click **Add to N channels**.
4. If the **Review Channel Associations** window opens, select the CRM company for each channel, or click **None**. Click **Save**.
5. Optional: turn on **Auto-add Ergo to Slack Connect channels**.
6. For the pre-call DM, each person opens **Settings → User integrations**, clicks the gear in the **Slack** row, and turns on **Pre-call context DM**.
7. Optional: turn on **Automatic pre-call brief**. Click **Edit prep template** to change what the DM says.
8. Test with one channel and one external meeting.

## What to expect

- With **Slack monitoring** on, Ergo reads the channels that it is in and updates deals with what it finds. The setting applies to everyone in the organization.
- To open **Review Channel Associations** later, go to **Slack settings → Channel associations → Manage**. Owners and team Admins see this row.
- Ten minutes before each external meeting, Ergo sends you a Slack DM. It shows the attendees, the last touchpoint and the open items.
- **Automatic pre-call brief** posts the brief in the thread of that DM.
- Pre-call context is extra context. It is not a full record of every Slack message.

## Common issues

- Ergo is not in the channel: add it with **Pick channels**. See [Add Ergo to new Slack customer channels](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/5430278897).
- Monitoring is off even though Slack is connected: turn on **Slack monitoring**.
- **Pick channels** is off: connect your own Slack account first.
- No pre-call DM: check **Pre-call context DM**, your calendar connection, and that the meeting is external.
- Channel names are out of date: see [Slack disconnect/reconnect and stale channel mappings](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/7906621776).
