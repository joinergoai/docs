Slack has two connections. The Owner or an Admin connects the workspace. Then each person connects their own Slack account.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** or a team **Admin**, with a **Standard** seat, connects the workspace.
- Each person with a **Standard** or **Notetaker** seat connects their own account. A **Spectator** seat cannot.
- Other people cannot connect the workspace. Ergo says that only admins can.

## Before you start

- Use a Slack workspace where your team approved Ergo.
- The workspace install does not put Ergo in your channels. You add Ergo to channels in a later step.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Org integrations**. In the **Workspaces** section, click **Connect** in the **Slack** row.
2. Approve the Ergo app in Slack. Ergo sends you back to **Org integrations**.
3. In the **Enable Slack Monitoring** window, click **Enable** or **Skip**.
4. Make sure that the **Slack** row shows **Connected** and the name of your Slack workspace.
5. Connect your own account: open **Settings → User integrations**. In the **Slack** section, click **Connect** and approve. Ergo needs this for DMs and approvals.
6. To add Ergo to channels, go back to **Org integrations** and click the gear in the **Slack** row. At **Channels Ergo watches**, click **Pick channels**. Select the channels and click **Add to N channels**.

## What to expect

- Before the workspace is connected, the user row reads "Your admin needs to connect Slack first".
- **Pick channels** is off until you connect your own Slack account.
- The switch **Auto-add Ergo to Slack Connect channels** lets Ergo join new Slack Connect channels with customers by itself.
- Review **Slack monitoring** (gear on the **Slack** row in **Org integrations**) and **Pre-call context DM** (gear on the **Slack** row in **User integrations**) if your team uses them.
- Ergo uses AI to summarize and show customer context in Slack. AI answers can be wrong, incomplete or old.

## Common issues

- Ergo is in the workspace but not in a channel: add the channel with **Pick channels**.
- **Pick channels** is off: connect your own Slack account first.
- Channel policy blocks the bot: ask your Slack admin.
- Slack stopped working: see [Google/Microsoft/Slack reconnects](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/5648164253) and [Slack disconnect/reconnect and stale channel mappings](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/7906621776).
