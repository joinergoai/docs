Gong has two parts. The organization **Owner** connects Gong one time. Then each person adds their own key, so that Ergo gets their calls.

## Who can use this

- **Part 1:** the organization **Owner** only.
- **Part 2:** each person with a **Standard** seat.

## Before you start

- One person can use only one notetaker. Gong cannot go with the Ergo Notetaker, Ergo Desktop, Granola or a different third-party notetaker. Ergo shows **Disconnect {name} first** if you try.
- Each person needs a Gong automation rule that sends their calls to a webhook.

## Steps

1. **Owner:** open **Settings → User integrations → Note takers**. On the **Gong** row, click **Connect for your organization**. (Or **Settings → Org integrations → Org-wide recording → Gong → Connect**.)
2. **Owner:** sign in to Gong and approve the access.
3. **Each person:** open **Settings → User integrations → Note takers → Gong** and click **Connect**.
4. In the dialog **Connect to Gong**, click **Copy** next to the **Webhook URL**.
5. In Gong, open your automation rule that sends your calls to a webhook. Use the URL that you copied.
6. In Gong, copy the Signed JWT Header public key of that rule.
7. In Ergo, paste the key in **Public Key** and click **Connect**.
8. Make sure that the **Gong** row shows **Disconnect**. Then record one test call in Gong and look for it in **Meetings**.

## What to expect

- Before the Owner connects Gong, other people see **Ask your org owner to connect Gong first** and no button.
- On **User integrations**, a connected row has no **Connected** label. The button changes to **Disconnect**.
- The Owner sees **Connected** in **Settings → Org integrations → Org-wide recording**.

## Common issues

- **Ask your org owner to connect Gong first**: the Owner must do part 1.
- **Disconnect {name} first**: disconnect your other notetaker, then connect Gong.
- Calls do not arrive: the Gong rule does not send to the **Webhook URL** from Ergo, or the public key is from a different rule.
