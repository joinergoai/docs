After Ergo processes an external sales meeting, it logs a meeting activity in HubSpot. Use this page to find it and to see why it can be missing.

## Who can use this

- Anyone who can open the deal, company or contact in HubSpot.
- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins** connect HubSpot. See [HubSpot](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/7579052434).

## Before you start

- Connect HubSpot. In **Settings → Org integrations → CRM**, the **HubSpot** row must show **Connected**.
- Make sure that the meeting is in Ergo and that processing is complete.

## Steps

1. Open **Meetings** and open the meeting. Check that processing is complete.
2. In HubSpot, open the deal, the company or a contact of the meeting.
3. Find the meeting activity with the title of the meeting.
4. Open the activity. The link **View Recording** is at the top of the notes.
5. Click **View Recording**. The meeting opens in Ergo. Sign in to Ergo if Ergo asks.
6. If the activity body is empty, look for a separate note on the same records.
7. If there is no activity, check that the meeting had external attendees and was a sales meeting. If it was, check which deal and company Ergo matched. Then contact Ergo support.

## What to expect

- There is no setting or workflow for this. Ergo logs the activity by itself.
- The destination is fixed. Ergo creates or updates one meeting activity with the outcome Completed. It adds the activity to the matched deal, company and contacts.
- **View Recording** opens the meeting page in Ergo. The reader must sign in to Ergo and must have access to the meeting.
- If you turn on a CRM note template (**Settings → CRM Settings → CRM Note Template**), the activity body is empty. Ergo then adds a separate note with the template content and the links.
- Ergo does not log internal meetings or meetings that are not sales meetings.

## Common issues

- The meeting was internal or was not a sales meeting. Ergo does not log it in HubSpot.
- The meeting matched a different deal or company. See [How Ergo names and matches CRM records](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/5841172398).
- The meeting is not processed. See [Meeting processing time and status states](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3507520478).
- The link does not open: the reader has no access to the meeting. See [Permission or access denied](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4190135592).
