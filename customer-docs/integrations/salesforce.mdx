Connect Salesforce when it is your CRM source of truth for deals, accounts and contacts.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- Only these roles see **Org integrations** and can connect or disconnect the CRM.

## Before you start

- Use a Salesforce account that can access the objects, fields, owners and pipeline data that Ergo must use.
- An organization can have only one CRM connected. If a different CRM is connected, disconnect it first. Until you do, **Connect** in the **Salesforce** row is off.
- Check your Salesforce validation rules and stage gates before you allow automatic updates.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Org integrations**.
2. In the **CRM** section, click **Connect** in the **Salesforce** row.
3. Approve the Salesforce access request.
4. Return to **Org integrations**. Make sure that the **Salesforce** row shows **Connected**.
5. Open **Settings → CRM Properties** and **Settings → CRM Stages**. On each page, click **Sync From CRM**. Select the properties and stages that Ergo must use.
6. Open **Settings → CRM Settings → Permissions**. Set **Allow creation** and **Allow updates** for each CRM object.
7. Test with a small number of meetings or deals.

## What to expect

- After you approve, a message can say that live sync is on for only some Salesforce objects. The other objects sync each night.
- After Ergo processes an external sales meeting, it adds an Event with the subject **Meeting Notes** to the opportunity. The description has the meeting link, a **Recording** link and the meeting notes. You do not configure this.
- The **Recording** link opens the meeting in Ergo. The reader must sign in to Ergo.
- Ergo does not log internal meetings.
- Salesforce rules can block a write. Ergo does not override Salesforce controls.

## Common issues

- A message says that live sync could not be enabled: contact Ergo support.
- The account you approved cannot access an object, owner or field: connect again with a different account.
- A validation rule blocks a stage or field update: change the rule or the Ergo setting.
- A picklist value or field type changed after setup: sync the properties again.
- Updates look wrong: see [CRM sync issues](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6503095041).
