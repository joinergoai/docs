The Ergo Notetaker is the built-in cloud bot that joins meetings from your calendar and records them.

## Who can use this

- People with a **Standard** or **Notetaker** seat. Each person connects the notetaker for their own meetings.
- A **Spectator** seat cannot connect a notetaker.
- The **Owner** sets the notetaker name, image and key terms for the whole organization.

## Before you start

- Connect your calendar first. Open **Settings → User integrations → Email & calendar** and connect **Google Workspace** or **Microsoft 365**. **Connect** for **Ergo Notetaker** stays off until you do.
- Decide which notetaker you use. You cannot connect Ergo Notetaker while Gong, Fireflies, Circleback, BlueDot, Fathom or Grain is connected. Ergo shows **Disconnect {name} first**.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → User integrations**.
2. In **Note takers**, find **Ergo Notetaker**. Click **Connect**.
3. In the **Connect Ergo Notetaker?** window, click **Connect**.
4. Wait until the row shows **Disconnect**. Then the notetaker is connected.
5. Click the gear icon on the row. In **Ergo Notetaker settings**, set **Send Summary Email** and **Transcription language**. Changes save by themselves.
6. Open the **Dashboard**. In **Upcoming Meetings**, check that your meetings show. Schedule or cancel the notetaker for each meeting.
7. To send the notetaker to a meeting that is not on your calendar, click **Add Bot to Meeting**.

## What to expect

- **Transcription language** is **English** or **Auto-detect (multi-language)**.
- The **Owner** opens **Settings → Org integrations → Ergo Notetaker → Notetaker identity** and clicks the gear icon to change the name, image and key terms. Other people see these values and the hint "Only your org owner can change this."
- Each person sets only their own summary email options and language.
- You can use Ergo Notetaker and Ergo Desktop Notetaker together.

## Common issues

- **Connect** is off: connect your calendar first.
- **Disconnect {name} first** shows: another notetaker is connected. Disconnect it, then connect again.
- The bot did not join: see [Notetaker did not join](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2299804002) and [Notetaker waiting-room admission guide](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4381304803).
- Two sources recorded one meeting: see [Using multiple notetakers with Ergo](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3534048190).
