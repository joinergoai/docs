Use Ergo CRM when your team wants contacts, companies and deals to live in Ergo and not in an external CRM.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**. Only these roles can connect or disconnect a CRM.
- The **Ergo CRM** row shows only for some organizations. If you do not see it, contact Ergo support.

## Before you start

- Decide that Ergo, and not an external CRM, is the main place for your deal records.
- An organization can have only one CRM connected. If an external CRM is connected, disconnect it first.
- Ergo CRM does not use a third-party token or an OAuth grant.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Org integrations**.
2. In the **CRM** section, find the **Ergo CRM** row.
3. Click **Connect**. There is no sign-in step.
4. Make sure that the message **Ergo CRM connected** shows and that the row shows **Connected**.
5. Open **Deals**. Check that your deals, companies and contacts are there.
6. Open **Settings → CRM Settings → Permissions** and **Settings → CRM Properties**. Review them before you rely on Ergo to write records.

## What to expect

- While Ergo CRM is connected, the **Connect** button of HubSpot, Salesforce and Attio is off.
- To change to an external CRM, click **Disconnect** in the **Ergo CRM** row first. Then connect the other CRM.
- Do not use the reconnect guidance for external CRMs for Ergo CRM.

## Common issues

- The **Ergo CRM** row is missing: it is not turned on for your organization. Contact Ergo support.
- You cannot connect an external CRM: Ergo CRM is still connected. Disconnect it first.
- A person cannot see or change records: check their role and seat. See [Permission or access denied](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4190135592).
- Your team has not agreed which CRM owns the deals. Decide this before you start.
