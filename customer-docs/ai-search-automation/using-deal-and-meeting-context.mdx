title: "Using deal and meeting context"
description: "Point Ergo at one deal or meeting so that it answers from that record."

Context helps Ergo answer a narrow question about one deal or meeting. It does not replace a check of the record itself.

## Who can use this

- Users with a Standard seat who ask about a specific deal, company or meeting.
- Users with a Notetaker seat can ask about a meeting from the meeting page.

## Before you start

- Make sure that you can access the record.
- Check that recent data has synced before you rely on it.

## Steps

1. Open the deal or the meeting.
2. Click the round Ergo button at the bottom right, or press ⌘J (Ctrl+J on Windows). The **Ask Ergo** panel opens.
3. Make sure that the pill above the text box shows the name of the deal or meeting.
4. Type a question about this record.
5. Read the sources and each proposal card before you approve a change.
6. When you open a different deal or meeting, click **Switch to <name>** above the text box. Ergo starts a new chat for that record.

## What to expect

- In **Chat with Ergo**, type **@** and two or more characters to select a deal or a Knowledge Base document.
- You cannot select a meeting with **@**. Open the meeting page.
- A meeting that Ergo still processes, or a disconnected integration, can give partial context.
- Ergo can summarize or propose changes from context. Check them before you send or update a record.

## Common issues

- Ergo answers from the wrong deal or meeting. Click **Switch to <name>** or start a new chat.
- Recent activity has not synced yet.
- A source is missing because it is outside your access.
- You assumed a broad answer is about the visible record, but no context was selected.
