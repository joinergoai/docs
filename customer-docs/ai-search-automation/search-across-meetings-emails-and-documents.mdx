Search opens a box where you can find pages, actions, deals, companies, meetings and chats.

## Who can use this

- Everyone. You see only the records that you have access to.

## Before you start

- To find words that someone said in a meeting, the meeting must have a transcript.

## Steps

1. Click **Search** at the top of the left navigation, or press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows).
2. Type the name of a page, an action, a deal, a company, a meeting or a chat. Type two or more characters to search records.
3. Use the tabs to show one type: **All**, **Pages**, **Deals**, **Companies**, **Meetings**, **Chats**.
4. Press ↑ or ↓ to move. Press Enter to open the result.
5. To search in one page, use the box on that page: **Search meetings...** on **Meetings**, **Search deals...** on **Deals**, **Search drafts...** on **Drafts**, **Search transcript...** in a meeting.
6. To find content in emails or Knowledge Base documents, ask in **Chat with Ergo**.

## What to expect

- The **All** tab shows the first 5 records. Open a tab, for example **Deals**, to see up to 10.
- A meeting can match in its title, its attendees or its transcript (**In transcript**).
- Email threads and documents are not search results.

## Common issues

- A record is missing: type more of the name, or open the tab for that type.
- A meeting is missing: you do not have access to it. See [Seats, roles and visibility](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6821509431).
- You want to filter meetings by a person: open **Meetings** and use its filters.
