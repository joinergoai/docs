Use **Chat with Ergo** to ask questions and get help with your work. Ergo answers from the sources that you can access.

## Who can use this

- Users with a Standard seat.
- A user with a Notetaker seat can ask Ergo only about one meeting, from the meeting page.

## Before you start

- Make sure that the records you expect are connected.
- Start a new chat when you switch to a different subject.
- Do not put private customer data in a prompt unless you may use it.

## Steps

1. Click **Chat with Ergo** in the left navigation.
2. Click **New chat**.
3. Type your question in the **Ask anything** box.
4. To point Ergo at a deal or a Knowledge Base document, type **@** and two or more characters of its name. To add a file, click **+** → **Add photos & files**.
5. Read the answer. Open the sources below the answer.
6. If Ergo proposes a change, read the card. Click **Approve** to apply it. Click **Reject** to cancel it.
7. To find an old chat, click the **Search chats** icon at the top of the chat list.

## What to expect

- Answers use only the sources and permissions that you have.
- On other pages, click the round Ergo button at the bottom right, or press ⌘J (Ctrl+J on Windows), to open **Ask Ergo**.
- A card that shows **Waiting for you** is a change that Ergo proposes. You can change a value on the card. Nothing changes before you approve.
- Ergo can propose a CRM update, a calendar event or an email draft. Review customer-facing text before you send it.
- A missing or old source can make an answer incomplete.

## Common issues

- Ergo uses the wrong context: you stayed in an old chat. Click **New chat**.
- Source data is missing or not indexed yet.
- You cannot see data that your role or seat does not allow.
