The **Ask Ergo** panel lets you ask Ergo questions while you stay on a deal, a meeting or another page.

## Who can use this

- Users with a Standard seat, on all pages where the panel shows.
- Users with a Notetaker seat, on a meeting page only (basic version).

## Before you start

- Open the record that you want to ask about, for example a deal or a meeting.

## Steps

1. Click the round Ergo button at the bottom right, or press ⌘J (Ctrl+J on Windows). The **Ask Ergo** panel opens.
2. Make sure that the pill above the text box shows the name of the deal or meeting.
3. Type a question, or click one of the prompts.
4. Read the answer, the sources and each proposal card.
5. For a different subject, click **New chat** (pencil icon). If you opened a different deal or meeting, click **Switch to <name>** above the text box. For an old chat, click **Thread history** (clock icon).
6. Click **Close** (X) at the top of the panel, or press ⌘J again.

## What to expect

- The panel takes context from the page that you are on.
- The button does not show on **Chat with Ergo** and **Drafts**.
- To make the panel narrower or wider, drag its left edge. On a small screen the panel uses the full width.
- The panel has no collapse control. Use **Close**.
- Proposal cards work as in **Chat with Ergo**. Nothing changes before you approve.

## Common issues

- The panel uses the context of an earlier record. Click **Switch to <name>** or **New chat**.
- The panel hides page content on a small screen. Close it.
- You do not see the button: your seat does not include the panel, or you are on a page without it.
