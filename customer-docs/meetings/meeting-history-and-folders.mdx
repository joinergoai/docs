Use **Meeting History** to find completed meetings and processed desktop or uploaded recordings that you can view. In the Ergo desktop app, the list also shows your notes.

## Who can use this

- Anyone who can open **Meetings**. The list shows only the meetings that you have access to.
- The organization **Owner** sees all meetings, except private meetings that the Owner did not attend.
- A team **Admin** sees the meetings of the people in the teams that they administer, and of the people granted to them.

## Steps

1. Open **Meetings**.
2. Type in **Search meetings...**. Ergo searches the title, the attendees, the company and the transcript. A result shows where the match is, for example **In transcript**.
3. Select **Filter** to add a filter: **Owner**, **Participants**, **Companies**, **Labels**, **Title contains**, **Transcript contains**, **Date**, **Meeting length**, **Outcome** or **Audience**.
4. To open a folder or a view, use the **Folders** panel at the left: **All Meetings**, **Private meetings**, **Smart Views** and **Libraries**.
5. Open a meeting to see the video, transcript, summary and more.

## What to expect

- The row shows **Desktop**, **Mobile** or **Upload** for a recording that did not come from the bot.
- The row shows **N captures** when more than one recorder captured the same meeting.
- A folder can give access to its meetings. When you share a folder with a person or a team, they can open all the meetings in it. Before you confirm, Ergo shows which meetings they will get.
- A **Company library** folder is visible to everyone. It opens its meetings only when an Owner turns on **Everyone can open meetings** in the folder settings.
- Private meetings stay private in all folders.
- A share link does not add the meeting to other people's **Meeting History**. The **Share with your organization** switch in the **Share Meeting** dialog does.

## Common issues

- You cannot find a meeting: you may not have access. See the article on availability and visibility.
- A meeting is missing: ask the Owner if **Time-Based Deletion** is on.
