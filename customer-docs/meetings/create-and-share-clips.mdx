Use a clip to share one part of a meeting instead of the full recording.

## Who can use this

- Anyone who can open the meeting. The meeting must have a recording.

## Steps

1. Open the meeting and open the **Video** tab.
2. Below the video, select **Create Clip**. Or select the text that you want in the transcript and select **Create Clip**.
3. Set the **Start** and **End** times. To join more parts of the meeting into one clip, select **Add another section**.
4. Enter a name for the clip.
5. Select **Save Clip**. From the transcript, select **Save only**, or select **Create & copy link** to save the clip and copy a link in one step.
6. Open the **Clips** tab to play, edit, download or delete the clip.
7. To share the clip, select **Copy link**.
8. To see the link again, add a password or revoke the link, select **Sharing settings**.

## What to expect

- **Copy link** makes the share link and copies it.
- To add a password, select **Sharing settings → Add Password Protection**. Enter a password of 6 to 128 characters. To remove it, select **Remove**.
- A clip link is separate from a full meeting link.
- To revoke a clip link, select **Sharing settings → Revoke Share Access**. This does not delete the clip. It does not revoke the meeting link.
- **Delete clip** is permanent and also revokes the clip link. You cannot restore a deleted clip.

## Common issues

- You do not see **Create Clip**: the meeting has no recording.
- A viewer cannot open the clip: the link was revoked, or the viewer needs the password.
