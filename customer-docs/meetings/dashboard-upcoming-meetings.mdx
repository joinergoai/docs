Use the **Dashboard** in the web app to make sure that Ergo sees an upcoming meeting and that the notetaker will join it.

## Who can use this

- People with a **Standard** seat or a **Notetaker** seat.
- A Spectator seat does not get the list. The card shows **Spectator access**.
- The desktop app and the phone app do not show the notetaker toggle.

## Before you start

- Connect your calendar in **Settings → User integrations → Email & calendar**.
- Connect the notetaker in **Settings → User integrations → Note takers**. Select **Ergo Notetaker**, then **Connect**. The notetaker toggle shows only after this step.
- Make sure that the calendar event has a meeting link, such as Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams.

## Steps

1. Open **Dashboard** in the web app.
2. Find the **Upcoming Meetings** card. The list shows 2 days at a time. Click the arrows to see more days.
3. Find the event. Check the title, the time and the attendees.
4. Click **Join** to check the meeting link.
5. Check the toggle. **Bot will join** means that the bot is scheduled. **Bot won't join** means that it is not.
6. From 15 minutes before the start time, check the bot status. Click **Remove Bot** if the bot must leave.

## What to expect

- The list shows only events that have a meeting link and are not finished.
- The bot status is **Joining...**, **Waiting Room**, **In Meeting**, **Recording** or **Bot Removed**.
- The status updates every 30 seconds.
- The calendar menu shows the calendar of your connected account.
- The **Meetings** page is **Meeting History**. It shows past meetings only.

## Common issues

- A meeting is missing: the calendar is disconnected or the connection expired.
- A meeting is missing: the event has no meeting link.
- A meeting is missing: someone made or changed the event just before the start time.
- No toggle shows: **Ergo Notetaker** is not connected.
