Ergo gives each new meeting a label, for example **Sales** or **Internal**. Ergo uses the description of each label to choose it.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins** can change the labels.
- Everyone sees the labels on their meetings.

## Default labels

- **Sales:** a buyer, prospect or customer conversation about buying the product or service.
- **Customer success:** an existing customer conversation about onboarding, support, renewal or expansion.
- **Hiring:** an interview or recruiting conversation with a candidate.
- **Internal:** a meeting where every participant belongs to the organization.
- **Other:** a meeting that fits none of the other labels.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Meetings**.
2. In **Labels**, click **Edit** on a label to change its name or description.
3. To add a label, click **Add label**. Write a description that tells Ergo when to use it.
4. To stop using a label, click **Archive**.

## What to expect

- You can have 20 active labels.
- A new or changed label applies to new meetings.
- Write each description so that two labels do not match the same meeting.
