Use **Ask AI** to ask questions about one meeting. Ergo answers with the meeting transcript and linked context.

## Who can use this

- A **Standard** or **Notetaker** seat: **Ask AI** opens the Ergo chat panel with this meeting as the context.
- A **Spectator** seat, and people who open a shared link: **Ask AI** opens a smaller chat panel for this meeting only.

## Before you start

- Make sure that the meeting has a transcript.
- Make sure that you are on the correct meeting if the meeting has more than one capture.

## Steps

1. Open **Meetings** and select the meeting.
2. Select **Ask AI** at the top right.
3. Type your question in **Ask about this meeting...**.
4. Read the answer. Review it before you use it outside Ergo.

## Good questions

- "What were the agreed next steps?"
- "What objections came up?"
- "Summarize the customer's main requirements."
- "Find quotes related to pricing."

## What to expect

- An answer can point to moments in the transcript. Select one to open the **Video** tab at that moment.
- People who open a shared link can also select **Ask AI**. That chat has a message limit for each shared meeting.
- When the limit is reached, the viewer sees "Chat limit reached for this shared meeting. Contact the person who shared it for more access."
- If a viewer sends messages too quickly, Ergo asks them to wait a few seconds.
