When **Recording Disclaimer** is on, the Ergo bot sends a chat message to all participants when it joins. The bot pins the message. The setting applies to the whole organization.

## Who can use this

- Only the organization **Owner** can see and change this switch.
- Members and team Admins must ask the Owner to change it.

## Before you start

- Connect your own **Ergo Notetaker** in **Settings → User integrations → Note takers**. The Owner sees the switch only after this step.
- Check your organization's recording-notice rules with your legal or compliance owner.

## Steps

1. Sign in to the web app as the Owner.
2. Open **Dashboard**.
3. In the **Upcoming Meetings** card, click the ⋮ button (**Calendar settings**).
4. Turn **Recording Disclaimer** on or off.
5. Make sure that the message "Recording disclaimer enabled" or "Recording disclaimer disabled" shows.

## What to expect

- The message is fixed: "This meeting is being recorded and transcribed by Ergo. For more information, please visit https://joinergo.com".
- A change also updates the bots that are already scheduled.
- The setting is a notice. It does not collect an acknowledgment from attendees.
- The setting does not replace your duty to follow consent laws or company policy.
- Desktop recording does not use the bot. Review that workflow separately.
- The setting does not change summary emails or meeting sharing.

## Common issues

- You do not see the switch: you are not the Owner, or your own **Ergo Notetaker** is not connected.
- A bot already joined before the change: check that meeting. The change may not have reached that bot.
- No chat message shows: make sure that someone admitted the bot, and that the meeting platform shows chat messages.
