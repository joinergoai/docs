Ergo can schedule the bot correctly and still miss a meeting if nobody admits it from the waiting room.

## Who can use this

- People with a **Standard** seat or a **Notetaker** seat. They see the bot status on the **Dashboard** in the web app.

## Before you start

- Tell the host that an Ergo notetaker may join.
- Make sure that the event link is the same link that attendees use.
- For an important meeting, keep **Dashboard** open in the web app. The meeting row in **Upcoming Meetings** shows the bot status. It updates every 30 seconds.

## Steps

1. Before the call, check that the toggle shows **Bot will join**.
2. During the call, watch the status on the meeting row.
3. If the row shows **Waiting Room**, ask the host to admit the bot.
4. If the row shows **Recording**, let the meeting end normally.
5. If the bot must leave, click **Remove Bot**.
6. If the bot never joined or left, use **Add Bot to Meeting** to send a new bot.

## What the states mean

- **Bot will join**: Ergo scheduled the bot. It is not in the call yet. The bot joins 3 minutes before the start time.
- **Joining...**: the bot is trying to enter the call.
- **Waiting Room**: the bot waits for someone to admit it.
- **In Meeting**: the bot is in the call.
- **Recording**: the bot is in the call and records.
- **Bot Removed**: a person removed the bot with **Remove Bot**.
- **Bot won't join**: someone turned the toggle off before the call.
- No status: the bot left the call.

## Common issues

- The bot leaves if nobody admits it in 33 minutes, if the host denies it, or if a person removes it.
- The bot leaves 2 minutes after the last participant leaves.
- The host is outside your organization: ask the host to admit the bot.
- Someone changed or made the meeting just before the start time: use **Add Bot to Meeting**.
