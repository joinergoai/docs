Use this when a meeting is live, last-minute, or missing from the upcoming list. The bot joins the call in a few seconds.

## Who can use this

- People with a **Standard** seat or a **Notetaker** seat. A Spectator seat cannot use this flow.

## Before you start

- Connect **Ergo Notetaker** in **Settings → User integrations → Note takers**. If it is not connected, the dialog shows a **Connect** button.
- Copy the full Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams meeting URL.
- This flow is for the Ergo bot. Desktop recording is a different way to capture a meeting.

## Steps

1. Open **Dashboard** in the web app. Click **Add Bot to Meeting** in the **Upcoming Meetings** card. Or open **Meetings** and click **New Meeting**.
2. In the **Add New Meeting** dialog, select the event in **Select Meeting**. If the event is not in the list, paste the URL in **Meeting Link**.
3. If you pasted a link, type a title in **Title (optional)**.
4. If you pasted a link, add the attendee emails in **Attendees**. Ergo needs them to run automations for the meeting.
5. Click **Dispatch Immediately**.
6. Make sure that the dialog shows **Notetaker Dispatched!**.
7. If the meeting has a waiting room, ask the host to admit the bot.

## What to expect

- Ergo adds your email automatically.
- If the link is the link of an event on your calendar that starts between 30 minutes ago and 4 hours from now, Ergo uses the title and attendees of that event.
- Each use sends one more bot. Do not use this when a teammate's bot is already in the call.
- After the call, find the recording on **Meetings**.
- If automations did not run, open the meeting and click **Push Automation**. Add the emails of the external attendees. Then click **Push to Automations**. This needs a Standard seat.

## Common issues

- The dialog says "Ergo notetaker not configured": connect **Ergo Notetaker** first.
- **Add Bot to Meeting** is disabled: connect your calendar.
- The bot does not enter: the meeting has a waiting room and nobody admitted it.
- The link does not work: use the full meeting URL.
