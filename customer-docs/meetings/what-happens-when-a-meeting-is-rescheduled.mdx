When you change a calendar event, Ergo updates the notetaker at the next calendar sync. A change close to the start time needs extra review.

## Who can use this

- People with a **Standard** seat or a **Notetaker** seat.

## Before you start

- Connect your calendar and **Ergo Notetaker** in **Settings → User integrations**.

## Steps

1. Wait for the calendar change to sync.
2. Open **Dashboard** in the web app. Find the meeting in **Upcoming Meetings**.
3. Check the time and the attendees.
4. If the link changed, click **Join** on the meeting row. Make sure that the new meeting opens.
5. Check that the toggle shows **Bot will join**.
6. If the bot did not move to the new link, turn the toggle off and then on.
7. If the meeting starts before the change syncs, use **Add Bot to Meeting** with the new link.

## What to expect

- Ergo moves the bot to the new link when the calendar change syncs.
- Do not change the meeting link while the bot is in the call. If the link changes, the bot leaves the call and Ergo schedules a new bot for the new link.
- For a recurring meeting, your calendar app asks if the change is for one occurrence or for all later occurrences. Ergo follows the calendar.
- Ergo asks its own question only when you click the notetaker toggle of a recurring meeting.
- If you turned the toggle off for an event, Ergo keeps that choice and does not add the bot again.

## Common issues

- The bot is still tied to the old link: turn the toggle off and then on.
- The meeting moved to a time too close to the start: use **Add Bot to Meeting**.
- The bot already joined at the old time: use **Add Bot to Meeting** to send a new bot.
