Turn on **Skip Internal Meetings** when you want the Ergo bot to skip calls where all attendees are from your company. The setting applies to your calendar only.

## Who can use this

- Each person with a connected **Ergo Notetaker**, for their own calendar.
- Each person on a team must turn it on for themselves.

## Before you start

- Connect **Ergo Notetaker** in **Settings → User integrations → Note takers**. The switch shows only after this step.
- Make sure that each extra domain of your company is in **Settings → Advanced → Domains → Alternate Domains**. The organization **Owner** or a team **Admin** can add a domain.

## Steps

1. Open **Dashboard** in the web app.
2. In the **Upcoming Meetings** card, click the ⋮ button (**Calendar settings**).
3. Turn **Skip Internal Meetings** on or off.
4. Look at the meetings in **Upcoming Meetings**. An internal meeting shows **Bot won't join**.

## What to expect

- Ergo always counts the domain of your email address as internal.
- A message shows how many scheduled internal meetings lost the notetaker.
- The setting changes bot scheduling only. It does not delete meeting records.
- If you turn the setting off later, the bot does not come back for the meetings that Ergo cancelled. Turn on the toggle for each of these meetings on **Dashboard**.

## Common issues

- A customer call is skipped: check that the attendees on the calendar event are correct and complete.
- An internal call is not skipped: its domain is missing in **Alternate Domains**.
- You do not see the switch: **Ergo Notetaker** is not connected.
