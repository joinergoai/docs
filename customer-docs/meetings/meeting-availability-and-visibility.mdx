Visibility decides who can find and open a meeting inside Ergo. Sharing decides who can open a link from outside. They are separate.

## Who can use this

- Everyone. The **Owner** manages the organization rules.

## Who can see a meeting

Ergo checks these rules in order and stops at the first match.

1. **Private meetings**: only the meeting owner and the internal people who attended. This overrides all rules below.
2. **Everyone can see all meetings** is on (**Settings → Meetings → Access**), or the viewer is the organization **Owner**: every meeting in the organization. Only an Owner sees this group.
3. **Team Admins**: their own meetings, the meetings of members of the teams they administer, and of the people granted to them.
4. **Everyone else**: meetings they owned or attended, plus the meetings of people listed under **Meeting access** in their member details (**Settings → Members**).

Also visible, subject to rule 1:

- Meetings in a folder that was shared with the viewer. A **Company library** folder opens its meetings only when an Owner turns on **Everyone can open meetings**.
- Meetings that the owner or an internal attendee shared with the organization (**Share → Share with your organization**). These viewers can find, view and ask questions. They cannot edit, delete or share. A private meeting cannot be shared this way.

Being on the same team as the owner does not give access. Only team Admins get team access.

## Private meetings

- **By hand**: on the meeting page, select **⋮ (More actions) → Make Private**. Only the owner and internal attendees see this command. Select **Make Public** to undo.
- **For the organization**: the Owner sets **Meeting Privacy Rules** in **Settings → Meetings → Privacy → Edit rules**, or in the **⋮ (More actions)** menu on **Meeting History**.
- **For yourself**: each person with a Standard or Notetaker seat can set **My Privacy Rules** in the same menu, or in **Settings → Profile → Meeting privacy → Edit rules**. A meeting that you are in becomes private when its title has one of your keywords, or when it includes a person that you picked.
- **Locked by policy**: some organizations keep certain meetings private. These show "This meeting is locked private by organization policy". You cannot make them public.

## Shared links

- **Share** creates a link that works outside these rules. The link can be password-protected or revoked.
- When a meeting becomes private, its links stop working.

## Common issues

- Someone cannot open a meeting. Check these points:
  - The meeting is private, by hand or by a rule.
  - The viewer is not the owner, an attendee, a team Admin, or a person with granted access.
  - **Everyone can see all meetings** is off.
  - The viewer got a clip link or a revoked share link.
- A teammate cannot see your recordings: check if a meeting title matches a privacy keyword.
