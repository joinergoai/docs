After you connect the Ergo Notetaker, Ergo schedules the bot for each calendar event that has a meeting link. Use the toggle to stop the bot for one event or to start it again.

## Who can use this

- People with a **Standard** seat or a **Notetaker** seat. A Spectator seat does not get the toggle.

## Before you start

- Connect your calendar and **Ergo Notetaker** in **Settings → User integrations**.
- Make sure that the event has a meeting link.

## Steps

1. Open **Dashboard** in the web app.
2. In the **Upcoming Meetings** card, find the meeting. The toggle is on the meeting row.
3. To schedule the bot, click the toggle when it shows **Bot won't join**.
4. To cancel the bot, click the toggle when it shows **Bot will join**.
5. For a recurring meeting, the **Recurring Meeting** dialog opens. Select **This meeting only** or **This and all future meetings**.
6. Click **Apply**. If you click **Cancel**, the toggle goes back.
7. If the row shows **Joining...**, **Waiting Room**, **In Meeting** or **Recording**, click **Remove Bot**. The row then shows **Bot Removed**.

## What to expect

- The label next to the toggle changes at once. If Ergo cannot schedule the bot, an error message shows and the toggle goes back.
- The default in the dialog is **This meeting only**.
- With **This and all future meetings**, Ergo schedules this meeting at once. It schedules the later meetings at the next calendar sync.
- When you cancel with **This and all future meetings**, Ergo cancels the bots that are already scheduled for the other meetings. You do not cancel them one by one.
- Removing the bot does not delete the calendar event.
- To send the bot to a meeting again, use **Add Bot to Meeting**.

## Common issues

- The bot does not join after you turn the toggle on: use **Add Bot to Meeting**. See the article on live or ad hoc meetings.
- One recurring meeting changed a different meeting: check which option you selected in the dialog.
