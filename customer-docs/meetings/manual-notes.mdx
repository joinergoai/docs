Use a manual note when you want a meeting record without a bot recording. Standalone notes are in the Ergo desktop app. The web app does not have them.

## Who can use this

- People with a **Standard** seat or a **Notetaker** seat, in the Ergo desktop app.
- In the web app, you can add your notes to a recorded meeting. Open the meeting, open the **Summary** tab and select **My Notes**.

## Steps

1. Open the Ergo desktop app.
2. Open **Meetings** and select **New Note**.
3. Enter a title in **Add title...**. Or select the date control (**Today**) and pick a calendar event. The event sets the title and the attendees.
4. To add a person, select the people control, select **Add**, and enter the email address.
5. Write in **Write your notes...**. The note saves automatically.
6. Optional: select **Add to folder**.
7. To share the note, select **Share**.
8. To send the note to your automations, select **Push Automation**, then **Push**.

## What to expect

- The page shows "Auto-saved".
- You cannot link a CRM record to a note. Ergo finds the CRM records from the attendees when you push the note.
- **Push Automation** shows only for a Standard seat. The note must be saved, have content, and have an attendee from outside your company.
- Only you can open your note in Ergo. The organization **Owner** and team **Admins** do not see your notes.
- **Share** makes a link for other readers. You can add a password.
- A deleted note cannot be restored.
- A manual note is not a bot transcript. The AI output depends on what you write.

## Common issues

- No **New Note** button in the web app: use the desktop app.
- No **Push Automation** button: you do not have a Standard seat, the note has no content, or it has no external attendee.
