The meeting page shows everything Ergo has for one completed meeting.

## Who can use this

- Anyone who can open the meeting. A person who opens a shared link sees only **Video** and **Summary**.
- **Push Automation** needs a **Standard** seat.

## Steps

1. Open **Meetings** and select a meeting.
2. Use the tabs to review the meeting.
3. Use the buttons at the top right for actions.

## Tabs

- **Video**: the recording, with the transcript beside it. Select a transcript line to go to that moment. Use **Search transcript...** to find words.
- **Summary**: **Action Items** (select the checkbox to mark an item complete), the **AI Summary**, and **My Notes**. A summary bar at the bottom makes a new summary.
- **Comments**: comments from you and your colleagues at a time in the recording.
- **Insights**: rubric grades, reporting mentions and talk time.
- **Clips**: the clips made from this meeting.

## Buttons at the top right

- **Ask AI**: ask questions about this meeting.
- **Push Automation**: send the meeting to the automations again.
- **Share**: share the meeting.
- **Download**: **Download Transcript** or **Download Video**.
- **⋮ (More actions)**: **Add to Folder**, **Make Private** or **Make Public**, **Exclude from Reporting** and **Delete Meeting**.

Below the title, you may see a link to the related deal (**Deal: …**) and to the post-call draft (**Follow-up Email**).

## What to expect

- An uploaded recording shows each speaker as **Speaker**. It starts with no summary and no action items. Use the summary bar on the **Summary** tab to make one.
- If a recording has no transcript, the **Video** tab shows "Recording complete — no transcript available."
- To see the meeting source, look at the row in **Meeting History**. It shows **Desktop**, **Mobile**, **Upload**, or **N captures**.

## Common issues

- The summary is empty: the recording may not have a transcript, or it is an upload.
- A person cannot open the meeting: check the visibility rules.
