When several people from one organization attend the same calendar meeting, Ergo sends one bot for the organization. Ergo does this when it schedules the bot from the calendar.

## Who can use this

- Anyone who asks why a teammate's bot joined, or why they see the same meeting.

## Before you start

- This rule applies to bots that Ergo schedules from the calendar.
- **Add Bot to Meeting** sends one more bot each time. Do not use it when the bot of a teammate is in the call.

## Steps

1. Open **Meetings**.
2. Use the **Owner** filter to find the owner of the meeting record.
3. Open the meeting to check the attendees.

## What to expect

- The owner can be a different teammate from the person whose calendar started the bot.
- The organization **Owner** sees all meetings.
- A team **Admin** sees the meetings of the team.
- Other people see meetings that they own or attended, and meetings that a person shared with them.
- If the Owner turns on **Settings → Meetings → Everyone can see all meetings**, each person sees all meetings.
- One recording can show for several teammates. This is normal.

## Common issues

- You get duplicate drafts or automation outputs: check how the meeting was captured. Two sources can capture the same call.
- A second bot joined: someone used **Add Bot to Meeting**.
- You cannot control the bot: seeing a meeting does not give you control of the bot.
