A finished recording does not mean that Ergo creates every output. Use this list to find the cause.

## Symptoms

- The meeting has a recording, but no follow-up draft.
- The meeting has no CRM update or insights.

## Causes and fixes

1. **Seat.** Ergo runs automations only if the meeting owner has a **Standard** seat, or if an attendee from your organization has one. A **Notetaker** or **Spectator** seat alone does not run them.
2. **Switch.** The owner turned off **Process my meetings, emails and Slack**. Check it in **Settings → Profile → Ergo on your account**.
3. **Missing data.** For a bot meeting, Ergo needs a transcript, a title and at least one attendee with an email address.
4. **Sales-meeting rule.** Ergo decides if each meeting is a sales meeting. If it is not, Ergo does not update the CRM from it.
   - The **Owner** or a team **Admin** sets the rule for each team in **Settings → CRM Settings → Meeting titles**.
   - Add the title phrases that identify a sales meeting.
   - Turn on **AI Boost** if Ergo must also use the meeting content.
   - If **AI Boost** is off and the list is empty, Ergo treats no meeting as a sales meeting.
   - A title with "internal:" or "internal-" is never a sales meeting.
5. **Privacy.** A meeting that your organization's privacy rules protect can get no downstream outputs.
6. **Drafts setting.** Make sure that **Post-call follow-up emails** is on in **Settings → Email Drafting → General**.
7. **CRM rules.** Check the CRM rules of your team in **Settings → CRM Settings → Permissions** and **CRM behavior**.
8. **Automation filter.** Each automation that starts from a meeting has a **Which meetings?** filter. Open the automation and check it. With no option selected, it runs on every meeting.

## Push the meeting again

1. Open the meeting in **Meetings**.
2. Click **Push Automation**. A Standard seat is necessary.
3. Add the emails of the external attendees. Click **Push to Automations**.

Do this one time. Ergo then runs the automations and does not apply the sales-meeting check. A meeting with the **Push Needed** badge has no attendee emails and needs this step.
