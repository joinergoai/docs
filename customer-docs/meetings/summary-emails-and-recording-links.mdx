After a call, Ergo can send a summary email. The email can include a button to open the recording and transcript.

## Who can use this

- Each person with a connected **Ergo Notetaker**, for their own setting.
- The organization **Owner** can set the default for all people. Team Admins do not see this switch.

## Before you start

- Connect **Ergo Notetaker** in **Settings → User integrations → Note takers**. The gear button shows only after this step.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → User integrations**.
2. In **Note takers**, find the **Ergo Notetaker** row. Click the gear button (**Ergo Notetaker settings**).
3. In **Your settings**, turn **Send Summary Email** on or off.
4. If the switch is on, set **Send Recording & Transcript**.
5. If the switch is on, set **Internal Recipients Only**.
6. Click **Done**. The switches save automatically.

## What to expect

- Ergo sends the email to all meeting participants, external participants included.
- Turn on **Internal Recipients Only** if only people in your company must get the email.
- Ergo sends the email only when the meeting has a summary and 2 to 9 attendees.
- For more than 9 attendees, Ergo sends the email only when **Internal Recipients Only** is on.
- Ergo does not send the email for a no-show or for a call with no business content.
- **Send Recording & Transcript** adds the **View Recording & Transcript** button. Ergo does not add it for a private meeting.
- The button opens the recording for the attendees only. The recipient confirms their email address with a one-time code.
- The Owner sets the default in **Dashboard → Upcoming Meetings →** ⋮ (**Calendar settings**) **→ Send Summary Email**. This overwrites the setting of each person with a connected notetaker. Each person can then change their own setting.

## Common issues

- No email arrives: the meeting has no summary, has fewer than 2 or more than 9 attendees, or **Send Summary Email** is off.
- The email has no button: **Send Recording & Transcript** is off, or the meeting is private.
- A person cannot open the link: they were not in the meeting.
