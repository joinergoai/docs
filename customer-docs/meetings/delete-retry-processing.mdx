Deleting a meeting is not the same as retrying a failed recording. Read both parts before you act.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** can delete each meeting.
- An internal attendee can delete a meeting only when an Owner turned on **Settings → Meetings → Allow internal attendees to delete the meeting**.
- Only the person who wrote a note can delete it.
- If you do not see the Delete command, you do not have permission.

## Steps

1. Open the meeting.
2. Select **⋮ (More actions) → Delete Meeting**. For a desktop recording, the command is **Delete Recording**.
3. In the confirmation, select **Delete Meeting**.
4. To undo the delete, select the undo button in the "Meeting deleted" message. You have 10 seconds.
5. To retry a failed desktop recording, find the row with "Processing failed" in **Meeting History**. Select **Retry**.
6. To retry an uploaded recording, use the card at the top of **Meeting History**. For "Processing failed", select **Retry**. For "Upload failed", select **Re-upload**.

## What to expect

- After 10 seconds, the meeting is not in Ergo and you cannot restore it in the app.
- If the meeting had more than one capture, Ergo deletes all of them.
- Ergo does not remove the recording files at once. To restore a meeting, or to remove its recording permanently, use the **Support** button.
- **Retry** shows only for a desktop recording or an uploaded recording that failed. A meeting that the bot recorded has no **Retry** button.
- Retry processes the captured data again. It does not send the bot back into a live meeting.
- A delete does not change CRM fields that Ergo already updated. Correct those in your CRM.

## Common issues

- No Delete command: ask your Owner to delete the meeting or to turn on the setting above.
- A failed recording has no **Retry**: it came from the bot. See the processing states article.
