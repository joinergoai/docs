Use the **Owner** filter in **Meetings** to show only the meetings of one rep or one team.

## Who can use this

- Anyone who can open **Meetings**. You see only the meetings that you have access to.
- The organization **Owner** sees all meetings, except private meetings that the Owner did not attend.
- A team **Admin** sees the meetings of the people in the teams that they manage.

## Steps

1. Open **Meetings**.
2. Click **Filter** and select **Owner**.
3. Select the rep, or select a team to see the meetings of all its people.
4. To narrow the list, add more filters, for example **Date** or **Companies**.
5. To find a meeting where the rep was only an attendee, use the **Participants** filter.

## What to expect

- **Owner** shows the meetings that belong to the rep. **Participants** shows the meetings that the rep attended.
- Private meetings show only to their owner and internal attendees.

## Common issues

- **The rep is not in the list, or meetings are missing:** you do not have access to the rep's meetings. Ask the Owner or the rep's team Admin to give you access in **Settings → Members → your name → Meeting access**. See [Grant meeting or reporting access](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3277963384).
- **A meeting is missing for everyone:** the notetaker did not record it, or it was deleted.
