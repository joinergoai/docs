Use this page when a setup step fails, or when a source, notetaker, or CRM setting seems to be missing after setup.

## Symptoms

- The setup wizard shows **Unable to verify onboarding status.**
- A step shows **This step couldn't load.**
- The wizard shows **Couldn't finish onboarding. Try again.**
- A connection, notetaker, or CRM setting is missing after you click **Finish**.
- Ergo does not process the meetings of a person.

## Causes and fixes

1. If the wizard shows **Unable to verify onboarding status.**, click **Try again**.
2. If a step shows **This step couldn't load.**, click **Skip step**. Complete that step later in **Settings**.
3. Each step is optional. **Back**, **Skip step**, and **Next** (or **Finish**) are always on. **Next** works when nothing is connected.
4. After you click **Finish**, the wizard does not open again. Make all later changes in **Settings**.
5. Open the **Dashboard**. If the card **Get Ergo working** shows, click **Connect** or **Turn on** on each open row. The rows are **Connect your CRM**, **Connect your calendar**, **Let Ergo join your calls**, and **Connect Slack**.
6. To connect your email, calendar, Slack account, or notetaker, open **Settings** → **User integrations**.
7. To connect the CRM, the Slack workspace, or a dialer, ask the organization Owner or a team Admin to open **Settings** → **Org integrations**. Members do not see this page.
8. For CRM updates, ask the Owner or a team Admin to complete **Settings** → **CRM Properties** and **CRM Stages**. Then open **Settings** → **CRM Settings** → **Permissions**. Make sure that **Allow creation** and **Allow updates** are on. If a page shows **Connect your CRM to continue**, connect the CRM first.
9. If Ergo does not process the meetings of a person, ask the Owner or the team Admin to open **Settings** → **Members**, click the person, and set **Automations** to on.

## Roles and seats

- Open **Settings** → **Members** → **Members**. Read the **Role** column (Owner, Admin, or Member) and the **Seat** column (Standard, Notetaker, or Spectator).
- A Spectator seat gets no setup wizard.
- Each person connects their own email, calendar, Slack, and notetaker.

## When to contact support

- Click **Support** in the left navigation.
- Give the step, the source system, the message that you see, and the time.
- Do not share sensitive customer content.
