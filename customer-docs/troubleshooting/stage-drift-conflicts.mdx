Stage drift happens when a stage changes in your CRM after setup. Ergo then shows a conflict on the stage row.

## Symptoms

- A stage row in Ergo shows **Resolve Conflict**.
- A stage row shows the badge **Not in CRM**.
- A new CRM stage is not in Ergo.
- A deal does not move to the stage that you expect.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**. A team Admin sees only the teams that the Admin manages.
- A **Member** does not see **CRM Stages**.

## Causes and fixes

1. Open **Settings** → **CRM Stages**. Select the team and the pipeline.
2. If a stage row shows **Resolve Conflict**, the stage has a different name in the CRM. Click **Resolve Conflict**. The window shows the **CRM Version** and the **Ergo Version** of the name.
3. Click **Keep CRM Version** to use the CRM name. Click **Update CRM** to rename the stage in the CRM (HubSpot and Attio only).
4. If **Update CRM** is off, change the stage name in the CRM. Then click **Keep CRM Version**.
5. If a stage row shows **Not in CRM**, the CRM does not have the stage now. Ergo removes a stage that the CRM deleted. You can also click the trash icon (**Delete stage**) on the row.
6. To add a new stage, click **Sync From CRM**. In **CRM Pipeline Stages**, select the stage and click **Sync Stages**. A stage that was made again in the CRM is a new stage. Add it in the same way.
7. Open the new stage row and write a description.
8. If the badge **Pipeline entry point required** shows, click **Stage Settings** and set an entry point.
9. Do a test with one deal.

## What to expect

- **Sync From CRM** only adds stages. It does not rename or remove a stage. If nothing is new, the button shows **No Changes**.
- A change of the stage order in the CRM is not a conflict.
- To delete a CRM stage from Ergo (HubSpot and Attio only), click **Sync From CRM** and open the **Edit** tab. Click the trash icon on the stage. If the stage has deals, move the deals in the CRM first. Changes on the **Edit** tab are written to your CRM.
