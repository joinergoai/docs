You had a call, but Ergo did not make a follow-up email draft. Check these causes in order. The first one is the most common.

## Who can use this

- Each person with a **Standard** seat. A Notetaker seat or a Spectator seat gets no drafts.

## Steps

1. **Turn on post-call drafts.** Open **Settings → Email Drafting → General**. Turn on **Post-call follow-up emails**. It is off for a new seat.
2. **Check that Ergo is on for you.** Open **Settings → Profile**. Make sure that **Process my meetings, emails and Slack** is on. When it is off, Ergo makes no drafts for you.
3. **Check the attendees.** Ergo drafts only after a meeting with an external attendee who has an email address. An internal meeting gets no draft.
4. **Check that the call was recorded.** Open **Meetings** and find the call. If it is not there, the notetaker did not record it. See [Notetaker did not join](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2299804002).
5. **Wait for processing.** A recording, a summary and a draft can finish at different times. Wait a few minutes after the call ends.
6. **Look in the other tabs of Drafts.** The draft can be in **Reminders**, **Scheduled**, **Composed** or **Errors**, or it was already sent or discarded.

## What to expect

- Ergo makes one post-call draft for each Ergo person on the call who has post-call drafts on.
- Ergo can decide that a meeting needs no follow-up. Then it makes no draft, also when all the settings are right.
- Ergo removes a post-call draft that you do not send after 7 days.

## Common issues

- **No drafts for any call:** step 1 or step 2 is the cause.
- **No draft for one call only:** step 3, 4 or 5 is the cause, or Ergo decided that no follow-up was needed.
