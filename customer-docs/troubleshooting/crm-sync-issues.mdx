Use this page when CRM fields or stages are missing, or when Ergo does not update the CRM as you expect.

## Symptoms

- A CRM field or stage is not in Ergo.
- A field row shows **Resolve Conflict** or **Not in CRM**.
- A field stays blank.
- Ergo does not create or update a deal, contact, or company.

## Causes and fixes

1. Ask the organization Owner or a team Admin to open **Settings** → **Org integrations**. In **CRM**, make sure that your CRM shows **Connected**. The connection is for the full organization.
2. Open **Settings** → **CRM Properties** and select the team. If the field is not in the list, click **Sync From CRM**, select the field, and click **Sync Selected Properties**. This only adds fields.
3. Look at the row of the field. If it shows **Resolve Conflict**, click it. Then click **Keep CRM Version** to use the CRM values, or **Update CRM** to send the Ergo values to the CRM.
4. If the row shows **Not in CRM**, the CRM does not have the field now. Restore the field in the CRM, or remove the row.
5. Read the write pill on the row. **Never writes** and **Read only in CRM** stop all writes. **Writes if empty** stops a write when the CRM field has a value. **Approval required** holds the value until a person approves it.
6. Open **Settings** → **CRM Settings** and select the team. On the **Permissions** tab, make sure that **Allow creation** and **Allow updates** are on for the object (Deal, Contact, or Company).
7. Open **Settings** → **CRM Stages**. Click **Sync From CRM** to add stages. If the badge **Pipeline entry point required** shows, click **Stage Settings** and set an entry point.
8. On the **Dashboard**, click **Recent activity**. In the **Activity Log**, set **Status** to **Failed** to find an update that did not work. Check the permissions of the CRM account that connected the CRM. Check that the CRM field accepts the value.

## Other checks

- Ergo manages the record owner fields. There is no owner sync.
- Ergo makes no updates for a person whose **Automations** switch is off in **Settings** → **Members**.
- Test with one known deal, contact, or company.

## When to contact support

- Click **Support** in the left navigation.
- Give the CRM, the object type, a link to the record, the field or stage, and the time.
- Do not share sensitive customer content.
