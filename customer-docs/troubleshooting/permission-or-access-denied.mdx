Ergo does not show an access denied page. A page that a person cannot use is not in the left navigation. Its link opens the **Dashboard**.

## Symptoms

- A page is not in the left navigation.
- A meeting shows **Meeting not accessible** or **Meeting access restricted**.
- A control in **Settings** → **Members** is off.
- The full screen shows **You don't have access to Ergo**.

## Causes and fixes

1. Open **Settings** → **Members** → **Members**. Find the person. Read the **Team**, **Role** (Owner, Admin, or Member), and **Seat** (Standard, Notetaker, or Spectator).
2. If **Automations**, **Drafts**, or **Chat with Ergo** is not in the left navigation, the person does not have a Standard seat. Only the organization Owner can change **Seat type**.
3. If **Reporting** is not in the left navigation, ask the Owner to click the person and click **Grant** in **Reporting access**.
4. If a meeting shows **Meeting not accessible**, ask the Owner or the team Admin of the person to click **Edit** in **Meeting access**. Then select the people and click **Save access**. A person cannot change their own meeting access.
5. To let all people see all meetings, the Owner sets **Everyone can see all meetings** to on in **Settings** → **Meetings**.
6. If **CRM Properties**, **CRM Stages**, or **Org integrations** is not in **Settings**, the person is not the Owner or a team Admin. The Owner can change the role to **Admin** in the **Memberships** of the person.
7. If a control in **Settings** → **Members** is off, hold the pointer on it to read the reason. Only the Owner can change the seat type, change team roles, and add people to teams.
8. The pages **Organization** and **Usage** show only to the Owner.
9. If the full screen shows **You don't have access to Ergo**, the account is not in an active Ergo organization. Ask the Owner to add the person in **Settings** → **Members**.

## What to expect

- A Member sees **Settings** → **Members** read-only.
- Private meetings are visible only to their owner and attendees.
- A shared link can give narrow access. See [Share, revoke, or password-protect meetings](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/7720202645).
- Do not share credentials to work around an access issue. Ask for the narrowest access that fits the task.

## When to contact support

- Click **Support** in the left navigation.
- Give the person, the page or meeting, the current role and seat, and the access that you expect.
- Do not share sensitive customer content.
