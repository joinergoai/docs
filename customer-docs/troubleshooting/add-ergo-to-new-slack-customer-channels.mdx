A new customer channel works with Ergo only after Ergo is in the channel. Use this page to add Ergo, or to let Ergo join new Slack Connect channels by itself.

## Symptoms

- A new Slack channel has no pre-call context or CRM updates.
- Ergo cannot find or post to the channel.
- The channel does not show in the channel list.
- An automation cannot use the channel.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins** open **Org integrations**.
- Each person connects their own Slack account in **User integrations**.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** → **Org integrations** → **Workspaces**. Make sure that **Slack** shows **Connected**.
2. Connect your own Slack account in **Settings** → **User integrations** → **Slack**. The channel list uses your account.
3. On the **Slack** row in **Org integrations**, click the gear. The dialog **Slack settings** opens.
4. Make sure that **Slack monitoring** is on.
5. To add Ergo to each new Slack Connect channel automatically, set **Auto-add Ergo to Slack Connect channels** to on.
6. Click **Pick channels**. In **Add Bot to Channels**, select the channel and click **Add to 1 channel**.
7. When **Review Channel Associations** opens, select the CRM company for the channel and click **Save**.
8. Send a new message in the channel as a test.

## Common issues

- **A private channel is not in the list.** Invite **@Ergo** in that channel in Slack. Then open **Pick channels** again.
- **A channel is not in the list.** Use **Search channels...** and the filters **All**, **Private**, and **External**. The list shows the public channels, the channels that Ergo is in, and the channels that you are in.
- **Pick channels is off.** Your own Slack account is not connected.
- **You do not see Org integrations.** Ask the organization Owner or a team Admin.
- **An automation cannot find the channel.** In the automation, type the channel name in the channel search. A public channel shows **Ergo will be added**. A private channel shows only after you invite **@Ergo**.
- Do not disconnect Slack to refresh the list. Ergo reads the list from Slack each time you open **Pick channels**. Close the dialog and open it again.
- Ergo connects to one Slack workspace for each Ergo organization. In a Slack Enterprise Grid, Ergo shows only the channels of that workspace.
- For a Slack Connect channel, make sure that your Slack workspace lets people add apps to the channel.
