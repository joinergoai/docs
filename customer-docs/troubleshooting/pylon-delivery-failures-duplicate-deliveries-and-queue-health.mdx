Ergo sends each completed meeting to Pylon as a call recording. The call recording has the transcript and a link to the meeting in Ergo. Ergo attaches it to the Pylon account of the customer, not to a ticket.

## Symptoms

- A call recording for the meeting is not in Pylon.
- Pylon shows two call recordings for one meeting.
- The meeting is still processing when you expect it in Pylon.

## Causes and fixes

1. Ask an organization admin to open **Settings** → **Org integrations** → **Workspaces**. Make sure that **Pylon** shows **Connected**.
2. If the Pylon API key changed, click **Update** and paste the new key.
3. Ask the organization Owner to click the gear on the **Pylon** row. In **Manage Pylon**, select **Everyone**, or select **Specific people**. Make sure that a person from the meeting is in the list.
4. Open the meeting in **Meetings**. Make sure that the meeting has a transcript and is not a **No Show**.
5. Make sure that the meeting is not private.
6. Make sure that the meeting owner has the **Ergo Notetaker** or **Fathom** connected in **Settings** → **User integrations**.

## What to expect

- Ergo selects the Pylon account from the email domains of the external attendees.
- Ergo sends each meeting to Pylon one time.
- Ergo tries again automatically when a send to Pylon fails. There is no retry button, no pause control, and no queue view.
- Ergo does not send a meeting that is still processing.

## When to contact support

- If the call recording is not in Pylon after these checks, click **Support** in the left navigation.
- If Pylon shows two call recordings for one meeting, click **Support**. Give the meeting name and the time.
- Do not share private message contents.
