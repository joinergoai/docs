Use this page when an expired Google, Microsoft, or Slack connection stops email, calendar, or Slack workflows.

## Symptoms

- Ergo shows the screen **Reconnect Your Google Account** or **Reconnect Your Microsoft Account**.
- You get an email from Ergo with the button **Reconnect on Ergo**.
- The row in **Settings** → **User integrations** shows **Expired**.
- An automation shows **Slack destination needs attention**.

## Causes and fixes

### Google or Microsoft

1. If the screen **Reconnect Your Google Account** or **Reconnect Your Microsoft Account** shows, click **Reconnect Google** or **Reconnect Microsoft**. Sign in. You cannot use other Ergo pages until you reconnect.
2. If no screen shows, open **Settings** → **User integrations** → **Email & calendar**. On the row with the pill **Expired**, click **Reconnect**.
3. Use the account that has the same email address as your Ergo sign-in. Ergo refuses a different address and shows **Please connect using the same email address you use to sign in to Ergo.**
4. You can connect Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, not both. To change provider, click **Disconnect** on the connected row first. The other row shows **Disconnect Google first** or **Disconnect Microsoft first**.
5. Read the email address on the provider row. If it is not correct, click **Disconnect** and connect the correct account.

### Slack

1. Open **Settings** → **User integrations** → **Slack**. If the row shows **Expired**, click **Reconnect**.
2. For the Slack workspace, ask the organization Owner or a team Admin to open **Settings** → **Org integrations** → **Workspaces**. If **Slack** does not show **Connected**, click **Connect**.
3. After a workspace reconnect, open **Automations**. Where **Slack destination needs attention** shows, click **Fix delivery**.
4. If an automation shows **Ergo is not in this channel**, click **Add Ergo to channel**. Or invite **@Ergo** in Slack.

Only Google and Microsoft have the full reconnect screen. Slack has no such screen.

## After you reconnect

- Do a test of one workflow.
- A workflow that failed before the reconnect may need a manual retry.

## When to contact support

- Click **Support** in the left navigation.
- Give the source system, the connected account, the workflow that you tested, and the time.
- Do not share sensitive customer content.
