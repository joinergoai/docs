Use this page when search finds nothing, or when a chart, a dashboard, or Reporting is empty.

## Symptoms

- The **Search** button (⌘K) shows **No results**.
- The meeting search shows **No results found**.
- A chart shows **No results match these settings.**
- The page shows **No dashboards yet**.
- **Reporting** is not in the left navigation.

## Causes and fixes

### Search

1. Click **Search** in the left navigation (⌘K). Type the name of a page, deal, company, meeting, or chat. This search does not look in emails or documents.
2. To search in transcripts and notes, open **Meetings** and use the search box. A hit in the content shows **In transcript** or **In notes**.
3. Start with one meeting that you know.
4. For an AI answer, open **Chat with Ergo** (Standard seat).

### Reporting

1. If **Reporting** is not in the left navigation, you do not have reporting access. The Owner and team Admins have access. A Member needs a grant.
2. Ask the organization Owner to open **Settings** → **Members**, click your name, and click **Grant** in **Reporting access**. The Owner selects **All dashboards** or **Specific dashboards**.
3. If a chart shows **No results match these settings.**, make the date range longer and remove the filters.
4. If a call grading chart shows **No scored calls match these settings.**, check the rubric and the filters.
5. If the page shows **No dashboards yet**, ask a reporting administrator to create a dashboard or to give you access to one.
6. If a test of a reporting email is empty, open **Reporting** → **Agent Reports**. Click **Test Run** on the report. Make sure that the period has meetings that Ergo processed.

## When to contact support

- Click **Support** in the left navigation.
- Give the search or chart name, the filters, an example that you expect, and the time.
- Do not share sensitive customer content.
