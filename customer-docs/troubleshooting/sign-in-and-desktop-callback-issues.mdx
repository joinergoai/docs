Use this page when a person cannot sign in to the dashboard or the Ergo desktop app, or stays signed out after the browser step.

## Symptoms

- The desktop app stays signed out after the browser shows **Sign-in complete**.
- The browser shows **Sign-in link expired** or **Sign-in did not complete**.
- The app shows **Waiting for authentication...** and nothing occurs.
- The screen **You don't have access to Ergo** shows after sign-in.
- The desktop app shows **Set Up Ergo Desktop** after sign-in.

## Causes and fixes

1. In the Ergo desktop app, click **Sign in** on the screen **Sign in to Ergo**. The browser opens.
2. Sign in with your work email in the browser. Use the same email that you use in the dashboard.
3. When the browser shows **Sign-in complete**, click **Open Ergo** in the browser prompt. If the app does not open, click the **Open Ergo** button on the page.
4. If the browser shows **Sign-in link expired**, go back to the desktop app. If the app shows **Waiting for authentication...**, click **Cancel**. Then click **Sign in** again.
5. If the browser shows **Sign-in did not complete**, click **Return to Ergo** and start again.
6. If you see **You don't have access to Ergo**, the email address is not in an active Ergo organization. Ask your admin to check the address in **Settings** → **Members**.
7. After sign-in, complete the list **Set Up Ergo Desktop**: **Microphone Access**, **Screen Recording**, **Accessibility**, and **Desktop Notetaker Connected**. Then click **Restart Ergo**.
8. To update the desktop app, open **Settings** → **Desktop app** → **About**. Read the version below **Ergo Desktop**. Click **Check for updates**. When the button shows **Restart to update**, click it.

## Other checks

- Close duplicate browser tabs for sign-in. Then start again from the desktop app.
- Keep the desktop app open while the browser finishes.
- Quit the app and open it again if it stays signed out.
- Try a clean browser profile. A browser setting, a single sign-on rule, or a network rule can block sign-in.

## When to contact support

- If you cannot sign in, ask a colleague who can sign in to click **Support** in the left navigation and report the problem for you.
- Give the email address, the desktop app version, the browser, and the time.
- Do not share sensitive customer content.
