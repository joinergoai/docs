Use this page when the Ergo bot did not appear in a meeting, was not admitted, or needs to join a live meeting.

## Symptoms

- The meeting starts but no Ergo bot appears.
- The bot is in the waiting room.
- The row shows **Bot Removed**.
- A meeting is not on your calendar, and you need the bot now.

## Causes and fixes

1. Open **Settings** → **User integrations** → **Note takers**. The **Ergo Notetaker** row must show **Disconnect**. If it shows **Connect**, click it.
2. If **Connect** is off, connect your Google or Microsoft calendar first in **Email & calendar**. Use the account that has the same email address as your Ergo sign-in. Ergo refuses a different address.
3. You can have only one notetaker. If the app shows **Disconnect <name> first**, disconnect that notetaker first.
4. Open the **Dashboard**. In **Upcoming Meetings**, find the meeting. If the row shows **Bot won't join**, click the switch. The text changes to **Bot will join**. For a recurring meeting, Ergo asks **This meeting only** or **This and all future meetings**.
5. If the meeting has only people from your company, click the three-dot button (**Calendar settings**) in **Upcoming Meetings**. Set **Skip Internal Meetings** to off.
6. During the meeting, read the state on the row: **Joining...**, **Waiting Room**, **In Meeting**, or **Recording**. If it shows **Waiting Room**, ask the host to admit the bot.
7. In **Upcoming Meetings**, click **Join** on the row to open the meeting link. If the link is not correct, add the bot with the correct link (step 8).
8. To send the bot now, click **Add Bot to Meeting** in **Upcoming Meetings**. Or click **New Meeting** on the **Meetings** page. Select the meeting in **Select Meeting**, or paste the link in **Meeting Link**. Click **Dispatch Immediately**.
9. If the row shows **Bot Removed**, do step 8 again to send a new bot.

## What to expect

- The Ergo Notetaker joins external meetings from your calendar.
- **Add Bot to Meeting** is off when the calendar is not connected or has expired. If your Google or Microsoft connection expired, Ergo shows **Reconnect Your Google Account** or **Reconnect Your Microsoft Account**. See [Google/Microsoft/Slack reconnects](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/5648164253).
- The desktop app dashboard does not have **Add Bot to Meeting**.
- Make sure that the event is on the calendar of the connected account.
- If another person's bot already covers a shared call, see [One bot per org: shared notetaking behavior](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/8920000915).

## When to contact support

- Click **Support** in the left navigation.
- Give the meeting title or link, the platform, the time, and the state of the bot (absent, waiting, admitted, or removed).
- Do not share sensitive customer content.
