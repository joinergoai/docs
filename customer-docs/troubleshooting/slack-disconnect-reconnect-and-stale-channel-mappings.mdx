Ergo has two Slack connections. Use this page when a reconnect works but channels or destinations still look wrong.

## Who can use this

- Each person can reconnect their own Slack account.
- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins** manage the Slack workspace and the channel associations.

## Before you start

- The **Slack workspace** is for the full organization. Your **Slack account** is only for you.
- A workspace disconnect stops Slack for all people. Do not disconnect the workspace unless Slack must stop for everyone.
- A person needs a Standard seat to connect the workspace.

## Steps

1. Find which connection has the problem.
2. Your account: open **Settings** → **User integrations** → **Slack**. Click **Disconnect**, then **Connect**. Sign in with your work Slack account.
3. Workspace (Owner or team Admin): open **Settings** → **Org integrations** → **Workspaces**. If the **Slack** row does not show **Connected** and the name of the correct Slack workspace, click **Connect**.
4. After a workspace reconnect, click the gear on the **Slack** row. In **Slack settings**, click **Pick channels**. Select the channels and click **Add to N channels**.
5. In **Slack settings**, click **Manage** in **Channel associations**. For each channel, select the correct company or click **None**. Click **Save**.
6. Open **Automations**. Where a row shows **Slack destination needs attention**, click **Fix delivery**. Select the correct channel, or click **Add Ergo to channel**. Then click **Refresh status**.
7. Do a test with one channel.

## What to expect

- An automation with a bad channel shows one of these: **Ergo is not in this channel**, **This Slack channel is archived**, **Ergo cannot access this channel**, or **Ergo is disconnected from Slack**.
- Reconnecting alone may not restore channel membership. Add Ergo to the channels again.
- After a reconnect, Ergo does not send again the automation runs that failed. Run those automations again if you need the output.

## Common issues

- **A channel points to the wrong company.** Fix it in **Channel associations**.
- **A channel was renamed, archived, or replaced.** Select the correct channel in the automation.
- **The Slack row does not show the right workspace.** Ask the Owner or a team Admin to reconnect it.
