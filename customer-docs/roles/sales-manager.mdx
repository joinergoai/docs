Use this page when a manager wants the shortest path to team visibility. Manager workflows work best after reps finish their own setup and RevOps sets up reporting.

## Who can use this

- Managers, team leads, founders and revenue leaders.
- An **Owner** gives Reporting access. An **Owner** or the team **Admin** gives meeting access.

## Before you start

- Make sure that the manager can see the team's meetings and **Reporting**.
- The simplest way: an **Owner** makes the manager an **Admin** of the team. Open **Settings → Members**, click the person, and use **Memberships**. A team **Admin** sees each meeting of the team and all dashboards.
- For a manager who stays a **Member**, use these controls in the same window:
  - **Meeting access** → **Edit**.
  - **Reporting access** → **Grant**. The **Owner** selects **All dashboards** or **Specific dashboards**.
- Tell reps how Ergo grades and health signals are used. Use them as coaching signals, not as automatic judgments.

## Steps

1. Open **Reporting**. Use the default dashboards to find patterns by rep, team or date range.
2. Open **Deals**. Start with the **High Priority** view. The other views are **Deals**, **This Week** and **Follow-Ups**.
3. Open a deal. Check health, recent activity and the next step.
4. Open a meeting. Read the summary, transcript, action items and call grade. Coach the behavior that you can point to.
5. Click **Chat with Ergo**. Name the team, rep, pipeline, date range or deal stage. Ask what needs attention. This needs a **Standard** seat.
6. Open **Automations** to run a regular summary. This needs a **Standard** seat.

## What to expect

- Call grades, deal health and reports are signals. Confirm them with meeting, CRM and rep context.
- Meeting outputs, grades and health signals can finish at different times.
- **Deals** shows for every seat. **Reporting** and its tabs show only with Reporting access.
- A manager with a **Notetaker** seat can ask about one meeting on its page only.

## Common issues

- The manager cannot see rep calls: team membership, meeting access or Reporting access is missing. See [Permission or access denied](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4190135592).
- A dashboard is empty: filters or the date range hide the data. See [Search/reporting has no results](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/8146112124).
- A meeting is missing: see [Notetaker did not join](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2299804002) and [Meeting processing time and status states](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3507520478).
- Grades look the same for every rep: ask RevOps to tighten the rubric.
- Do not use one score as the whole truth. Combine stage, close date, engagement and recent meetings.
