Use this page when you are a CSM, account manager or onboarding owner. It shows how to use Ergo for customer context and follow-up.

## Who can use this

- CSMs, account managers, onboarding owners and post-sale teams.
- Most workflows need a **Standard** seat.

## Before you start

- Finish the setup wizard. Connect your own email, calendar and notetaker.
- Ask your **Owner** or team **Admin** to set up the CRM fields, stages, templates and reports for post-sale work.
- Make sure that customer meetings are captured. Skip internal planning meetings unless your team wants them.

## Steps

1. Open **Deals**. Open the company or the deal of your customer.
2. Check recent meetings, summaries, action items, emails and documents.
3. Press ⌘K (or Ctrl K) to open **Search**. Find a company, deal or meeting by name.
4. Open **Chat with Ergo**. Name the customer and the date range. Ask about a topic, a commitment, a risk or what changed.
5. After a call, open **Drafts**. Check facts, owners, dates, links and tone. Then send or dismiss.
6. Open **Reporting** to see the default dashboards, if you have Reporting access.

## What to expect

- Summaries, transcripts, drafts, health signals and reports can finish at different times.
- Company records are inside **Deals**. The left navigation has no separate item for companies.
- **Search** finds pages, deals, companies, meetings and chats. It does not search email or documents.
- Chat answers are as complete as the sources that Ergo has.
- Review every AI draft and recap before you send it to a customer.

## Common issues

- A meeting is on the wrong record, or an internal call was captured: fix the source first. See [Skip internal meetings](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6629085842).
- A meeting has no insights or drafts: see [Why some meetings do not generate insights or drafts](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1207359797).
- You cannot open a page or report: see [Permission or access denied](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4190135592).
- Search or reports show nothing: see [Search/reporting has no results](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/8146112124).
- A draft does not send: see [Failed drafts retry/report](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2798745358).
