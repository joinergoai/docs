Use this page when you are an SDR or BDR. It gives a short path through Ergo for outreach, follow-up and handoff. Use the controls that you see in your workspace as the source of truth.

## Who can use this

- SDRs, BDRs and sales development managers.
- Most workflows need a **Standard** seat.

## Before you start

- Finish the setup wizard. Connect your email, calendar and notetaker.
- If your team uses a dialer, ask an **Owner** or team **Admin** to connect it. See [Outreach](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4534096844), [Nooks](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6672173844) or [Salesfinity](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2216734899).

## Steps

1. Open **Deals**. Start with the **Follow-Ups** view to see who needs attention.
2. Open a deal. Read recent activity and past meetings before a discovery call.
3. Open **Chat with Ergo**. Ask which prospects need attention and what happened recently.
4. After a call, open **Drafts**. Review each follow-up draft. Then send or dismiss.
5. For a batch of follow-ups, use bulk drafts. See [Bulk email drafts](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2766924623).
6. Before you hand off a deal, ask Ergo what the AE needs to know.
7. Open **Automations** to run a regular task, such as a weekly follow-up list.

## What to expect

- Drafts are for you to review. Ergo does not send them for you.
- Press ⌘K (or Ctrl K) to open **Search**. It finds pages, deals, companies, meetings and chats.
- Ergo keeps CRM records current only for the fields and permissions that your team set up.
- Some workflows depend on your organization setup.

## Common issues

- A draft does not send: see [Failed drafts retry/report](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2798745358).
- Search or reports show nothing: see [Search/reporting has no results](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/8146112124).
- Email or Slack stopped working: see [Google/Microsoft/Slack reconnects](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/5648164253).
- No drafts after a meeting: see [Why some meetings do not generate insights or drafts](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1207359797).
- You cannot open a page: see [Permission or access denied](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4190135592).
