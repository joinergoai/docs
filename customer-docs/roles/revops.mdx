Use this page when you prepare Ergo for a team. Make the shared setup reliable before people judge meetings, drafts, CRM updates or reports.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins** who do the shared setup.
- Some pages are for the **Owner** only, such as the **Reporting access** grant.

## Before you start

- Choose the CRM that is your source of truth.
- Decide which workflows are in scope for a pilot. For example: meeting capture, CRM updates, drafts, Slack, reports.

## Steps

1. Sign in for the first time. The setup wizard opens. It has up to 15 steps, from **Connect your CRM** to **Configure reporting**.
2. Do the wizard first. A team **Admin** sees the same list without **Connect your CRM** and **Connect your dialer**.
3. Review CRM setup in **Settings → CRM Properties**, **CRM Stages** and **CRM Settings**.
4. In **CRM Stages**, click **Stage Settings**. Set **Entry Point**, **Lock Stage** and **Status** with sales leadership.
5. Add people in **Settings → Members → Add Member**. Pick the seat and the team.
6. Give Reporting access: open **Settings → Members**, click the person, and click **Grant** next to **Reporting access**.
7. Run a small pilot. Test one meeting, one draft, one CRM update and one report.

## What to expect

- CRM setup is per team. A change applies to each person in that team.
- **Copy configuration** in **Settings → CRM Settings** replaces a team's setup. You cannot undo it.
- A person who clicks **Skip step** in the wizard misses only their own connection or setting. They can do it later in **Settings**.
- You do not map the CRM record owner field. Ergo sets the owner.
- An **Owner** and team **Admins** have all dashboards. A **Member** needs a grant.

## Common issues

- CRM updates look wrong: check **CRM Properties** and **CRM Stages**. See [CRM sync issues](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6503095041) and [Stage drift conflicts](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1509454679).
- A person cannot see **Reporting**: check Reporting access. See [Dashboard setup errors](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3951667254).
- Duplicate drafts: more than one notetaker records the same meeting. See [Using multiple notetakers with Ergo](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3534048190).
- A person cannot open a page: see [Permission or access denied](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4190135592).
- Check AI output and CRM writes with a small sample before you roll out to everyone.
