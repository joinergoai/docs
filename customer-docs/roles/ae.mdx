Use this page when you are an account executive and want the shortest path to daily selling work with Ergo.

## Who can use this

- Account executives and account owners with a **Standard** seat.

## Before you start

- You connect your own email, calendar and notetaker. You do this in the setup wizard at your first sign-in. It has 6 steps.
- Your organization and your team set up the CRM connection, CRM properties and stages. You do not set them up.

## Steps

1. Finish the setup wizard. Connect your email and calendar, your notetaker and Slack. Set the switches in **Email and drafting**.
2. Open the **Dashboard**. Check **Upcoming Meetings**.
3. If the bot is not in a live or ad hoc call, click **Add Bot to Meeting**. Make sure that the host admits the bot.
4. After a call, open **Drafts**. Review the recipients, facts, dates and tone. Then send or dismiss.
5. Open **Deals**. The board has the built-in views **High Priority**, **Deals**, **This Week** and **Follow-Ups**.
6. Open **Chat with Ergo**. Name the customer, deal, meeting or date range. Ask for call prep, risks or next steps.
7. Use **Search** (⌘K) to find a deal, company, meeting or chat.

## What to expect

- Ergo drafts are for you to review. Ergo does not send them for you.
- A recording, a transcript, a summary and a draft can finish at different times.
- Ergo writes mapped CRM changes when the integration, permissions and approvals are set up.
- Two notetakers for one meeting can make duplicate drafts. Pick one.
- **Add Bot to Meeting** does not work until your calendar is connected.
