Use **Compose** to start an email yourself. You do not need to wait for Ergo to make a draft.

## Who can use this

- People with a Standard seat and a connected email account.

## Before you start

- Connect your email account. See [Connect email and calendar](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4928954637).
- Have the recipient and any thread context ready.

## Steps

1. Select **Drafts** in the left navigation, then click **Compose**.
2. Fill in **To**, **Subject** and the body. Click **Cc/Bcc** to add those recipients.
3. To reply in an existing thread, click the thread in **Related Threads** on the right. To go back to a new email, click **Exit Thread**.
4. To get help with the text, type an instruction in the **Email Assistant** box at the bottom of the draft.
5. Click **Send**, or click the arrow next to **Send** to schedule the email.

## What to expect

- Ergo saves the draft as you type. There is no Save button.
- The email goes from your connected email account. A **From** row shows only when your organization has org-wide draft access.
- A composed email starts a new thread.
- The draft editor has no template menu.
- The draft shows in the **Composed** tab until you send it.
- You can edit AI text before you send.

## Common issues

- The email account is disconnected. Reconnect it, then try again.
- A required field is empty: **To**, **Subject** or the body.
- You selected the wrong thread. Recipients see the email in that conversation. Click **Exit Thread** to start a new one.
