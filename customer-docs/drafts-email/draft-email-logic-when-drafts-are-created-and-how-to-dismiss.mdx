Ergo makes drafts only from set triggers. Not every meeting or message gets a draft.

## Who can use this

- People with a Standard seat who want to know why a draft did or did not appear.
- Owners and team Admins who tune draft settings.

## Before you start

- Make sure that your email and calendar are connected.
- Check if the draft is in another tab, such as **Reminders**, **Scheduled**, **Composed** or **Errors**.

## Draft sources

- **Post-call drafts**: Ergo makes one draft for each Ergo user on the call when all of these are true:
  - **Post-call follow-up emails** is on for that person (**Settings → Email Drafting → General**).
  - The meeting has one or more external attendees with an email address.
  - Ergo decides that the meeting needs a follow-up email.
  - That person does not have a post-call draft for this meeting already.
- **Inbox reply drafts**: when **Email inbox flow (triage)** is on, Ergo drafts replies to customer threads in your inbox.
- **Automation drafts**: an automation with the **Draft email** destination makes a draft for the sender to review.
- **Composed drafts**: you start these with **Compose** or with Ergo chat.
- **Quote follow-ups** and **account handoffs**: only when these features are on for your organization.

## Discard a draft

1. Open the draft.
2. Click **Discard draft** (the X button at the bottom left of the draft), or press **E**.
3. To get the draft back, click **Undo** in the message. The message shows for 10 seconds.
4. To close the deal also, click the arrow next to **Discard draft** and then **Disqualify**. Ergo moves the deal to Closed Lost in your CRM. Do this only when the deal is lost.

## What to expect

- Discarding a draft does not send an email.
- A missing draft can mean that the meeting did not qualify, processing is not complete, or Ergo decided that no follow-up is needed.
- New activity can make an older draft stale. Ask the **Email Assistant** in the draft to update the text, or discard the draft.
- Ergo removes a post-call draft that you do not send after 7 days.
- **Retry** on the **Errors** tab sends the same email again. It does not make a new draft.

## Common issues

- **Post-call follow-up emails** is off.
- The draft is already discarded, sent, scheduled or set to a reminder.
- The draft is attached to a different deal, meeting or thread than you expected.
