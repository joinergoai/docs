Use a template for an email pattern that you use again. To change one draft only, edit the draft.

## Who can use this

- Each person with a Standard seat can make personal templates in **My Templates**.
- Only the Owner or an Admin of a team can make or change the templates of that team.

## Before you start

- Decide who uses the template.
- Write a clear name and description.
- Remove customer-specific details from examples.

## Steps

1. For a personal template, open **Settings → Email Drafting** (below **Your settings**) → **Templates**. Keep **My Templates** selected.
2. For a team template, open **Settings → Email Drafting** (below **Team**). Select the team and go to **Post-call email templates**.
3. Click **Create Template** (personal) or **Create template** (team). To change a template, click the edit button on its row.
4. Fill in **Template Name**, **Template Description** and **Template Content**. Put [brackets] where Ergo must write text, for example [next steps].
5. To get help with the text, click **Ask Ergo** in the content box.
6. For a new template, click **Create template**. After that, Ergo saves each change automatically.
7. Add attachments if you want. Ergo adds them to each draft that it makes from the template.
8. Click **Done**. To delete the template, click **Delete template** and confirm.

## What to expect

- The name and the content are necessary for a new template.
- Do not add a sign-off. Ergo adds your email signature.
- A change applies to future drafts. It does not change drafts that exist.

## Common issues

- The description does not say when Ergo must use the template.
- The content has old names, dates or customer-specific examples.
- You expect an edited template to change drafts that exist.
