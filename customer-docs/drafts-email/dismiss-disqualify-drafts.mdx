**Discard draft** removes one draft. **Disqualify** also moves the linked deal to Closed Lost in your CRM. Use **Disqualify** only when the deal is lost.

## Who can use this

- People with a Standard seat who work through drafts.

## Before you start

- Decide what is wrong: the text, the timing, or the deal.
- To fix the text, edit the draft or use the **Email Assistant**. Do not discard it.
- To use **Disqualify**, connect your CRM. Your CRM pipeline must have a stage with "Closed Lost" in its name.

## Steps

1. Open the draft.
2. To remove only the draft, click **Discard draft** (the X button at the bottom left of the draft) or press **E**. In a tab list, point at a row and press **E**.
3. To get the draft back, click **Undo** in the message.
4. To lose the deal also, click the arrow next to **Discard draft** and then **Disqualify**. Or press **D** and confirm.
5. Open the deal afterward. Make sure that the stage is correct.

## What to expect

- Discarding a draft does not send an email.
- You can get a discarded draft back only with **Undo**. The **Undo** message shows for 10 seconds.
- **Disqualify** moves the linked deal to the Closed Lost stage in your CRM and discards the draft.
- **Disqualify** is not available when the draft has no linked deal.
- The shortcuts are the same in all tabs and in Go Mode: **E** discards the draft, **D** disqualifies the deal (with a confirm step). Click the keyboard button at the top of a draft to see all shortcuts.

## Common issues

- You discarded a draft that only needed an edit.
- You disqualified a deal that should stay open. Move the deal back in your CRM.
- **Disqualify** shows an error: the pipeline has no stage with "Closed Lost" in its name. The draft comes back.
- The draft is for a different deal or person than you expected.
