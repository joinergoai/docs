Review each AI draft as you would review any email you write. Edit it before you send it.

## Who can use this

- People with a Standard seat who send email from Ergo.

## Before you start

- Open the draft from the **Drafts** page.
- Read the meeting, deal or thread that the draft is about.

## Steps

1. Check the **To** field. Click **Cc/Bcc** to see those rows when they are empty. A **From** row shows only when your organization has org-wide draft access.
2. Check **Subject**. A reply shows the messages of the thread around the reply box and has no **Subject** row. Use **Related Threads** on the right to change the thread.
3. Edit the body in the editor.
4. Add, remove or check the attachments and inline images.
5. Click **Signature** below the body to see the signature and the **Sent with Ergo** footer. Use the signature menu to select **Default**, **No signature**, or another signature for this email.
6. Click **Send**, the arrow next to it (**Schedule send**), the bell (**Remind me later**), or the X (**Discard draft**).

## What to expect

- AI text can miss detail, overstate a next step or use the wrong tone. Edit it before you send.
- Some drafts show related threads. Use them to avoid a repeat of an earlier email.

## Common issues

- The draft uses old meeting or deal information. Edit the text or discard the draft.
- The AI selected an old thread, or no thread. Choose the right thread in **Related Threads**.
- A limit is exceeded: 10 attachments, 25 MB in total for one email, or 5 MB for one pasted image.
- No signature is saved. Add one at **Settings → Email Drafting → Personalization → Email signatures**, or click **Add signature** in the **Signature** bar of the draft.
