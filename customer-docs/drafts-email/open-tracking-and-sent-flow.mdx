Open tracking shows if a person opened an email that you sent from Ergo. Use it as a signal. It is not a full record of delivery or interest.

## Who can use this

- People with a Standard seat who review emails they sent.

## Before you start

- Send the email from Ergo.
- Ergo tracks opens for each email that you send from Ergo. There is no setting to turn it on or off.

## Steps

1. Select **Drafts** in the left navigation.
2. Select the **Sent** tab. It shows the 100 most recent emails that you sent from Ergo.
3. Look at the mark on each row. One grey tick means sent and not opened. Two green ticks mean opened. Point at the mark to see the number of opens and the times.
4. Look at the **Recent Opens** panel on the right of the **Drafts** list. It lists the latest open for each thread. Click an entry to open the sent email.
5. If the **View email flow** button shows next to the tabs, click it to see **Sent email flow**. Then click a group to filter the **Sent** list.
6. Use the search box to find an email by subject, body text or recipient.

## What to expect

- Open tracking shows that an email was opened, how many times, and when the latest open was. Ergo does not track link clicks.
- The **Sent** tab has no opened or unopened filter.
- **View email flow** shows only for some organizations.
- Open tracking can miss opens or add false opens. Email clients, proxies, previews and security scanners can change the result.
- Tracking does not show which person opened an email.
- A scheduled email shows in **Scheduled**, not in **Sent**, until it is sent.

## Common issues

- No opens show: the recipient's email client can block tracking.
- An open shows that a preview or a security scanner caused.
- You cannot find an email: it is older than the 100 most recent. Use the search box, or look in your own mailbox.
- You sent the email outside Ergo. It does not show in the **Sent** tab.
