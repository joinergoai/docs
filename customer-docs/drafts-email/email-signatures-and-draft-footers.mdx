Signatures control what Ergo adds to the end of your emails. Set them up so that drafts do not show a sign-off two times.

## Who can use this

- People with a Standard seat who send email from Ergo.
- **Email Drafting** shows in Settings only for a Standard seat.

## Before you start

- Connect your email account. See [Connect email and calendar](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4928954637).
- Decide if you import your Gmail signature or type one.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Email Drafting** (below **Your settings**) → **Personalization**. Go to **Email signatures**. Or, in a draft, click **Edit** in the **Signature** bar.
2. Click **Import from Gmail**. It shows only when a Google account is connected.
3. Or click **Add signature**, type a **Signature name** and the text, and click **Save**. For Microsoft 365, paste the signature here.
4. Select the star (**Set as default**) on the signature that goes on each new draft.
5. To remove a signature, click the delete button on it and confirm.
6. Turn **Sent with Ergo** on or off. The switch is below the signature list.

## What to expect

- You can keep up to 25 signatures. The signature with the **Default** label goes on each new draft.
- In a draft, use the signature menu to select **Default**, **No signature** or another signature for that email.
- You cannot edit a signature with the **Gmail** label in Ergo. Edit it in Gmail and click **Import from Gmail** again. Edit a signature that you added in Ergo with **Edit signature**.
- Ergo cannot import a signature from Outlook.
- When **Sent with Ergo** is on, Ergo adds the Ergo footer to the emails that you send.
- For a Google account, **Include signature in Gmail drafts** adds your default signature to the Gmail drafts that Ergo makes.
- A signature change affects future emails. It does not change sent emails.

## Common issues

- The signature shows two times: your mail client adds its own signature to drafts. Turn off **Include signature in Gmail drafts**.
- You added a sign-off in the body and also have a saved signature. Remove one.
- **Import from Gmail** does not show: connect Google Workspace first.
- No signature shows in a draft: no signature is saved. Add one.
