Go Mode opens the drafts of several deals that you selected. Go Mode does not send drafts in bulk. You send each draft one at a time.

## Who can use this

- People with a Standard seat who can select deals in **Deals → List**.

## Before you start

- Make sure that 2 or more deals have a draft ready.
- Check that the deals you select are the right ones.

## Steps

1. Open **Deals** and select **List**.
2. Select the checkbox of 2 or more deals that have a draft ready.
3. Click **Go Mode** at the top of the **Draft** column. **Drafts** opens in a new browser tab with only the **Go Mode** tab.
4. Review the draft that opens. The first draft opens automatically.
5. Send, schedule, edit, discard or disqualify. Each action applies to this draft only.
6. Press **K** to open the next draft.

## What to expect

- Drafts in Go Mode work like other drafts. Send, schedule, discard and checks are the same.
- Shortcuts: Cmd/Ctrl + Enter sends, **S** schedules, **E** discards, **D** disqualifies the deal (with a confirm step), **K** opens the next draft, **J** opens the previous draft, and Esc goes back to the list. Click the keyboard button at the top of a draft to see the list.
- Check the recipients, the body and the thread of each draft before you send.

## Common issues

- A draft was sent, discarded or removed after you selected the deal. If no draft is left, Ergo shows "No matching ready drafts were found for those selected deals."
- You sent too fast without a check of the thread. Use **Undo** within 10 seconds.
- You used **D** when an edit was safer. **D** moves the deal to Closed Lost.
