By default, you see only your own drafts. Org-wide draft access lets people see the drafts of the whole organization. Ergo support turns it on for an organization.

## Who can use this

- Owners and admins who want to know who can see drafts. Admins cannot change draft visibility in Ergo.
- People with a Standard seat who need to see or send a draft for a teammate.

## Before you start

- Ask Ergo support to turn on org-wide draft access for your organization. There is no setting for it in Ergo. See [Get support](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2708243049).
- No role is necessary. When org-wide draft access is on, each person in the organization who can open **Drafts** can see the drafts of all other people.

## Steps

1. Select **Drafts** in the left navigation.
2. Find the owner filter next to the search box. It shows only when org-wide draft access is on.
3. Select **My Drafts**, **All Users**, or a person below **Team Members**.
4. Use the tabs and the search box inside that scope.
5. Select **My Drafts** again when you work on your own drafts.

## What to expect

- You can open, edit and send a draft of another person. The email goes from the email account of the draft owner. The **From** row in the draft shows the sender.
- A person with no connected mailbox shows "Workspace not connected".
- Templates and draft access are separate. Sharing a template does not share drafts.

## Common issues

- The owner filter does not show: org-wide draft access is not on for your organization.
- You see your own drafts only: the filter is on **My Drafts**.
- A draft does not send: the email account of the draft owner is not connected.
