After each external meeting, Ergo can draft a follow-up email for you. You turn this on or off for your own account.

## Who can use this

- Each person with a **Standard** seat, for their own account.
- An admin cannot change this switch for a team or for the organization.

## Before you start

- Connect your email and calendar. See [Connect email and calendar](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4928954637).

## Steps

1. Click **Settings** in the left navigation.
2. Below **Your settings**, click **Email Drafting**.
3. Click the **General** tab.
4. In **What Ergo does with your mailbox**, turn **Post-call follow-up emails** on or off.
5. The change saves at once. There is no **Save** button.
6. To test, hold a meeting with an external attendee. Then open **Drafts → Post Call**.

## What to expect

- A new Standard seat starts with **Post-call follow-up emails** off. Turn it on here or in the setup wizard step **Email and drafting**.
- Ergo does not send a post-call draft by itself. You review and send each draft.
- Ergo drafts only after external meetings.

## Common issues

- No drafts after a meeting:
  - **Post-call follow-up emails** is off.
  - **Settings → Profile → Ergo on your account → Process my meetings, emails and Slack** is off. This switch pauses all drafts for you.
  - The meeting had no external attendee.
  - You do not have a Standard seat.
- No **Email Drafting** page below **Your settings**: you do not have a Standard seat.
