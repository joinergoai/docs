Yes, Ergo learns from the emails you edit. It also learns your voice from the emails that you sent before. You can see what it learned and change it.

## Who can use this

- Each person with a **Standard** seat. What Ergo learns is for you only. It does not change the drafts of your teammates.

## How Ergo learns

- **Writing voice:** after you connect your email, Ergo reads up to 30 external emails that you sent and writes a short profile of your style. When you send drafts through Ergo, it updates the profile: after your first sends, at the third send, and then after each 10 sends.
- **Email rules:** when you change an Ergo draft before you send it, Ergo compares its draft with what you sent. From the difference it makes or confirms a rule, for example about how you start or end your emails. If you send a draft with almost no change, Ergo counts the rule as correct.
- **Links:** Ergo finds the links that you often share in your sent emails, so that drafts can use them.

## See and change what Ergo learned

1. Open **Settings → Email Drafting** and click the **Personalization** tab.
2. **Writing voice** shows **Active** when Ergo uses your style, or **Learning** until it has enough emails. Open it to read your profile. You cannot edit the profile.
3. In **Email rules**, open a rule to edit it, pause it, or delete it. You can also add a rule. Rules change or add to your writing voice.
4. **Links**, **Signatures** and **My post-call instructions** are on the same tab.

## What to expect

- The more drafts you send through Ergo, the closer the drafts get to your style.
- A paused rule is not used, but Ergo keeps it.
- Ergo does not send emails for you. You review and send each draft.

## Common issues

- **Writing voice stays on Learning:** send a few drafts through Ergo.
- **Drafts keep doing something you do not want:** add a rule in **Email rules**, or edit the rule that causes it.
