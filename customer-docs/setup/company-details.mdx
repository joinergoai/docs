Use **Company Details** to tell Ergo what your company does, which problems it solves and how. Ergo uses this text in drafts, summaries and CRM updates for the whole organization.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- A **Member** does not see this page.
- Company details are one record for the organization. They apply to all teams and all people at once. You do not copy them to people.

## Before you start

- Connect your CRM first (**Settings → Org integrations → CRM**). Until the CRM is connected, the fields are read only.
- Use wording that stays true across many customer calls. Do not use one account or one deal.

## Steps

1. Open the setup wizard step **Company details**, or open **Settings → Company Details**.
2. In the wizard, Ergo fills empty fields by itself one time. Read the text and correct it.
3. In **Company Description**, write one or two sentences about what you sell and to whom.
4. In **Challenges Addressed**, write up to three customer problems.
5. In **Solution Overview**, write up to three points about how you solve them.
6. Click out of the field or press Enter. Ergo saves by itself. There is no **Save** button. Press Shift+Enter for a new line.
7. To write the text again with AI, click **Generate with AI** (wizard) or **Ask Ergo** (**Settings**).
8. Test one workflow that uses company context, such as one follow-up draft.

## What to expect

- **Generate with AI** replaces the text that is there. Ergo asks you to confirm first.
- In **Settings**, **Ask Ergo** opens the Ergo agent. The agent researches your company and fills the fields.
- A change affects future output. Drafts and descriptions that Ergo already made do not change.
- Do not add customer names, account numbers, secret prices or claims that you have not approved.
- Update the text when your positioning or main use cases change.

## Common issues

- The fields are read only: the CRM is not connected. The page shows "Connect your organization's CRM to edit company details."
- Output sounds generic: write clearer challenges and solution points. Then test one draft again.
- Output uses old positioning: update the text, and check templates and stage descriptions.
- CRM updates are still wrong: company text does not fix mapping. See [CRM sync issues](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6503095041).
- Drafts are missing: see [Why some meetings do not generate insights or drafts](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1207359797).
