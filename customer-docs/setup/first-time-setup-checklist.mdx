Ergo setup is a wizard. It opens when a person signs in for the first time. Each role gets its own list of steps.

## Who can use this

- Everyone. The wizard shows the steps for your role and seat.

## Before you start

- The organization **Owner** does the wizard first. The Owner connects the tools that the whole organization shares (CRM, Slack workspace, dialer).
- Have your CRM admin account and your work email account ready.

## Steps

1. Sign in at dashboard.joinergo.com. The wizard opens. Click **Begin**.
2. **Owner:** connect the shared tools. Do **Connect your CRM**, **Connect your email and calendar**, **Connect Slack and Pylon**, **Set up your notetaker** and **Connect your dialer**.
3. **Owner:** tell Ergo about your company and your CRM. Do **Company details**, **CRM properties** and **CRM stages**. In **CRM stages**, turn on an **Entry Point** for each pipeline. Without it, Ergo does not create new deals.
4. **Owner:** do **Advanced settings**, **Deal and account health**, **Email templates** and **Knowledge base**.
5. **Owner:** do **Add your team**. Click **Add Member** for each person. Select the team, the role and the seat.
6. **Owner:** do **Automations**. If your organization has Reporting, do **Configure reporting**. Click **Finish**.
7. Each person signs in and does the wizard for their role (see the table below).
8. Record one external meeting as a test. Then check the summary, the draft and the CRM update.

| Role or seat | Wizard steps |
|---|---|
| Owner | 14 steps (15 with Reporting), as above |
| Team Admin | The Owner steps without **Connect your CRM** and **Connect your dialer** |
| Member | **Connect your email and calendar**, **Set up your notetaker**, **Connect Slack**, **Email and drafting**, **Knowledge base**, **Automations** |
| Notetaker seat | **Connect your email and calendar**, **Turn on the Ergo Notetaker** |
| Spectator seat | No wizard. The app opens at once. |

## What to expect

- The header shows **Set up Ergo**, your role and **Step n of N**. The footer has **Back**, **Skip step** and **Next** (**Finish** on the last step).
- **Next** marks a step done, also if you connected nothing. Use **Skip step** for a step that you do not need now.
- After **Finish**, the wizard does not open again. Make later changes in **Settings**.
- If a connection is missing after the wizard, the **Dashboard** shows the card **Get Ergo working**. Click the button in each row.

## Common issues

- A Member sees no CRM step. This is correct: the Owner or a team Admin connects the CRM for the organization.
- No new deals are created. Open **Settings → CRM Stages → Stage Settings** and turn on an **Entry Point** in each pipeline.
- A person skipped a step. Open **Settings → User integrations** (personal tools) or **Settings → Org integrations** (shared tools).
