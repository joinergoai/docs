Templates give Ergo a reusable structure for post-call follow-up drafts. Always review each draft before you send it.

## Who can use this

- A person with a **Standard** seat can make personal templates.
- A team **Admin** or the **Owner** can make team templates. Members see them read only.

## Before you start

- Connect your email and calendar. See [Connect email and calendar](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4928954637).
- Turn on post-call follow-up emails. See [Turn post-call drafts on or off](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3684399934).
- Decide which call types need a different structure. For example: discovery, demo or renewal.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Email Drafting → Templates**.
2. Make sure that **Use templates in generated emails** is on.
3. In the list at the top left, select **My Templates**. To see team templates, select your team.
4. Click **Create Template**.
5. Fill **Template Name**. In **Template Description**, write when Ergo must use this template.
6. In **Template Content**, write the email. Put the text that Ergo must write in [brackets]. Do not add a sign-off.
7. Click **Create template**. Later changes save automatically.
8. To get a first version or to improve a template, click **Ask Ergo** in the corner of **Template Content**.

To share a template with a team, a team **Admin** opens **Settings → Email Drafting** (in the **Team** group), selects the team, and clicks **Create template** below **Post-call email templates**. In the setup wizard this is the step **Email templates**.

## What to expect

- Write the description as a rule for when to use the template. Do not write it like the email.
- Ergo compares the templates to the meeting. If no template fits well, Ergo drafts without one.
- The template shapes the draft. The final text is not word for word.
- Ergo adds your email signature when it sends. Set it up in [Email signatures and draft footers](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/9446610633).
- A member gets the team templates automatically. The switch **Use templates in generated emails** turns all personal and team templates on or off for your drafts.
- Do not put private customer details in a template.

## Common issues

- The wrong template is chosen: the descriptions are vague or overlap. Make each description specific.
- The signature shows two times: remove the sign-off from **Template Content**.
- Drafts ignore templates: **Use templates in generated emails** is off, or no template fits.
- You cannot change a team template: only a team **Admin** can.
- No draft at all: see [Why some meetings do not generate insights or drafts](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1207359797).
- A template does not control tone. Use [Reprompt drafts with AI](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/7814089761) for tone and length.
