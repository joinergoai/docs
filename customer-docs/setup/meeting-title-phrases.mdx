Meeting title phrases help Ergo decide which calendar meetings are sales meetings. The setting is for one team.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**. A team **Admin** sees only the teams that the Admin manages.
- All members of a team use the same phrases. Members cannot change them.
- This setting is not in the setup wizard.

## Before you start

- Connect your calendar. See [Connect email and calendar](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4928954637).
- Look at the names that your team uses for customer meetings. For example: Demo, Discovery, QBR, Renewal.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → CRM Settings**.
2. Click the **Meeting titles** tab.
3. Select the team at the top right.
4. Leave **AI Boost** on if Ergo must use meeting content and your company details together with your phrases. Turn it off if only your phrases must decide.
5. In **Meeting title phrases**, type a phrase and press Enter.
6. To remove a phrase, click the **×** on it.
7. Test with one customer meeting and one internal meeting.

## What to expect

- Ergo saves each change at once. There is no **Save** button.
- A phrase matches anywhere in the title. Upper case and lower case are the same.
- Each new team starts with **AI Boost** on and no phrases. You do not have to change this for Ergo to work.
- With **AI Boost** off, a meeting is a sales meeting only if its title has one of your phrases. With no phrases, no meeting is a sales meeting.
- A title with "internal:" or "internal-" is never a sales meeting.
- Use short phrases that stay stable. Do not use broad words such as "sync" or "call". They can pull in internal meetings.
- **Copy configuration** does not copy meeting title phrases. Set them for each team.

## Common issues

- A customer call is missed: add a phrase that its title uses, or turn **AI Boost** on.
- Internal meetings produce output: remove broad phrases. See [Skip internal meetings](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/6629085842).
- A meeting has no draft or insights: see [Why some meetings do not generate insights or drafts](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/1207359797).
- The notetaker did not join: phrases do not control joining. See [Notetaker did not join](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2299804002).
- A new team names meetings in another way: add phrases for that team.
