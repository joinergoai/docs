Write one short text that describes how your company charges customers. Ergo uses it for deal amount extraction. It is not Ergo billing.

## Who can use this

- Only the organization **Owner** can read or change the pricing description.
- A team **Admin** sees the **Pricing** tab as read only. The tab says that only an Owner can change pricing.
- A **Member** does not see this page.

## Before you start

- Confirm your pricing model with your rollout owner.
- Do not put secret discounts, legal terms or one-time exceptions in the text.
- Map CRM amount, package and discount fields separately. See [CRM properties setup](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3371162277).

## Steps

1. Sign in as the **Owner**.
2. In the setup wizard, open the step **Advanced settings** and click **Pricing**. After setup, open **Settings → Advanced → Pricing**.
3. In **Pricing Description**, write how you charge customers. Include the price model, packages, units and tiers.
4. Click out of the field or press Enter. Ergo saves by itself.
5. Change the text when your pricing changes.
6. Test with a small number of deals.

## What to expect

- There is one text for the whole organization.
- The text helps Ergo extract deal amounts. It does not replace CRM amount fields, quote approvals or a billing system.
- The tab says: "Configure how your company charges customers for accurate deal amount extraction."
- If deal qualification is off for your organization, the **Owner** step in the wizard opens on **Pricing**.

## Common issues

- You cannot edit the text: you are not the **Owner**.
- Reports lack pricing data: the CRM amount, package or discount fields are not mapped.
- Amounts look wrong: the text is too broad, old or full of exceptions. Make it shorter and more exact.
- This page is not for public pricing. Keep public pricing and contract terms in your own commercial source.
