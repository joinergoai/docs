Select the deal, contact and company fields that Ergo reads and writes. For each field, tell Ergo what to put in it and when it can write.

## Who can use this

- The organization **Owner** and team **Admins**.
- Field settings are for one team. Select the team at the top right of the page.

## Before you start

- Connect the CRM first. See [Connect your CRM](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2772377599).

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → CRM Properties** (wizard step **CRM properties**). Select the team at the top right.
2. Click **Sync From CRM**. In the setup wizard this window opens by itself.
3. Use the tabs **Deal**, **Contact** and **Company**. **Custom** shows the fields that your company made. **CRM Defined** shows the standard fields of your CRM.
4. Click each field that Ergo must use. Keep **Auto-generate descriptions** selected. Click **Sync Selected Properties**.
5. Open each field. Read the **Description** and correct it, or click **Ask Ergo** for a draft.
6. Set **Write behavior**: **Ergo can always write to this field**, **Ergo will only write if not already populated**, or **Ergo will never write to this field**.
7. To make a new field, click **Add Property**, fill the form, and click **Create Property**. Then click **Push to CRM** on the row to add the field to your CRM.
8. Process one meeting and check the CRM record.

## What to expect

- **Deal Name** writes always. **Amount** and **Close Date** start as **Ergo will never write to this field**. Change them if Ergo must fill them.
- A field that you sync starts as **Ergo can always write to this field**.
- To have a person approve each value first, select **Require approval before writing** on the field.
- Changes save automatically.
- The sync window only adds fields. To remove a field, click the trash icon on its row.

## Common issues

- A field never updates: its **Write behavior** is **Ergo will never write to this field**, or the field is read-only in your CRM.
- The row shows **Incomplete**: add a description, or add options to a dropdown field.
- The row shows **Resolve Conflict**: the field changed in your CRM. Click it and choose which version to keep.
- A standard CRM field is missing: click **CRM Defined** in the sync window.
