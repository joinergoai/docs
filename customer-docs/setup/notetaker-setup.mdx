Choose how Ergo gets your meetings: the Ergo Notetaker (a bot that joins the call), the Ergo Desktop app, or a different notetaker.

## Who can use this

- Each person, for their own meetings.
- A **Standard** seat can use all notetakers. A **Notetaker** seat can use the Ergo Notetaker only. A **Spectator** seat cannot connect a notetaker.
- Only the **Owner** can change the bot name, image and key terms. They are the same for the whole organization.

## Before you start

- Connect your Google or Microsoft calendar first. The Ergo Notetaker **Connect** button is off until you do.
- One person can use only one notetaker. The Ergo Notetaker and the Ergo Desktop app can go together. Fireflies, Circleback, BlueDot, Fathom, Grain, Gong and Granola cannot go with the Ergo Notetaker or with each other. Ergo shows **Disconnect {name} first** if you try.

## Steps

1. Open the wizard step **Set up your notetaker**, or **Settings → User integrations → Note takers**.
2. On the **Ergo Notetaker** row, click **Connect**. In the window **Connect Ergo Notetaker?**, click **Connect**.
3. The button changes to **Disconnect**, and a gear icon shows.
4. Click the gear icon. Set **Send Summary Email** and **Transcription language** (**English** or **Auto-detect (multi-language)**).
5. **Owner only:** in the same window, below **Organization**, set **Notetaker name**, **Notetaker image** and **Key terms**. Or open **Settings → Org integrations → Ergo Notetaker → Notetaker identity**.
6. To record without a bot, click **Download** on the **Ergo Desktop Notetaker** row, then **Download for macOS**. Sign in to the app with your Ergo account. The web app has no **Connect** button for it.
7. For a different notetaker, click **Connect** on its row and paste the key or the token.
8. Open **Meetings** and make sure that your next external meeting shows. For a meeting that already started, use **Add Bot to Meeting** on the **Dashboard**.

## What to expect

- The Ergo Notetaker joins the external meetings in your calendar by default.
- **Send Summary Email** sends the summary to the meeting participants. When it is on, you can also set **Send Recording & Transcript** and **Internal Recipients Only**.
- The **Ergo Desktop Notetaker** row shows only when the desktop app is on for your organization.

## Common issues

- **Connect** is off on the Ergo Notetaker row: connect your calendar first.
- **Disconnect {name} first**: you already have a different notetaker. Disconnect it, then connect the new one.
- The bot does not join: see [Notetaker did not join](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2299804002).
