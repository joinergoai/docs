Use **Quick Note** in the Ergo desktop app to record an ad hoc conversation with a note. The recording is linked to the note.

## Who can use this

- People with a **Standard** seat or a **Notetaker** seat who use Ergo Desktop.

## Before you start

- Sign in to Ergo Desktop.
- Grant the macOS permissions. See the macOS permissions article.

## Steps

1. Open the Ergo desktop app. On the home page, select **Quick Note** (top right). A new note opens.
2. Type a title in **Add title...**. This is optional.
3. Select **Record** at the bottom of the note.
4. Type your notes in **Write your notes...** during the conversation.
5. To stop, select **Stop recording** (the square icon).
6. On the home page, look at **Recent**. Wait until **Processing...** goes away.
7. Open the meeting. Make sure that it has your notes and the transcript.

## What to expect

- The recording does not start by itself. You select **Record**.
- Ergo saves the note automatically.
- If you leave the title empty, Ergo writes a title after the recording ends.
- Each note can have one recording. To record again, select **Quick Note** for a new note.
- If a recording is in progress, Ergo asks "Stop recording and create a new note?" Select **Stop & Create Note**.
- Each recording that you start in the app opens its note. You do not link a note by hand.

## Common issues

- The row in **Recent** shows **Processing failed**: select **Retry**.
- **Record** is not available on the note: the note already has a recording.
