Ergo Desktop needs three macOS permissions to record: **Accessibility**, **Microphone** and **Screen Recording**. While one is missing, the app shows "Ergo can't record meetings on this computer yet".

## Who can use this

- Each person who uses Ergo Desktop on macOS.

## Before you start

- Install and sign in to Ergo Desktop.

## Steps

1. In the Ergo desktop app, open **Settings → Desktop app**.
2. In **System permissions**, find each permission that shows **Required**.
3. Select **Grant access** next to **Microphone**. Select **Enable access** next to **Accessibility** and **Screen Recording**.
4. In macOS **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, turn on **Ergo** in **Accessibility**, **Microphone** and **Screen & System Audio Recording**. If **Ergo** is not in a list, select **+** and add it.
5. Go back to Ergo and select **Restart Ergo**.
6. Make sure that all three permissions show **Granted**. Then make a short test recording.

## What to expect

- **Accessibility** finds active meetings and participants.
- **Microphone** captures meeting audio.
- **Screen Recording** records the meeting window. Ergo cannot record without it.
- The app also shows **Restart now** in the banner "Restart Ergo to apply new permissions".
- You cannot restart during a recording. The app says "Finish recording before restarting Ergo."
- While a permission is missing, the **Settings** item in the sidebar is marked.

## Common issues

- A permission still shows **Required**: restart Ergo.
- The permission is on, but Ergo does not record: make sure that the entry is **Ergo**, not another app.
- A company device policy blocks the permission: ask your IT team.
