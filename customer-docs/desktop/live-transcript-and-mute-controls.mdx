During a desktop recording, you can read the live transcript and mute the recording. Both controls are in the recording bar.

## Who can use this

- Each person who records a meeting with Ergo Desktop.

## Before you start

- Sign in to Ergo Desktop. See [Install and sign in to Ergo Desktop](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4290838215).
- Give the macOS permissions. See [macOS permissions](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4584568980).
- Make sure that your computer is connected to the internet.

## Steps

1. Start a recording in the Ergo desktop app. The recording bar shows at the bottom of the note.
2. Select the waveform button (**Expand transcript**). The panel **Transcript** opens.
3. Read the transcript. Your words are on the right. The words of other speakers are on the left, with the speaker name.
4. To mute the recording, select the microphone button (**Mute recording**). The recording card shows **Muted**.
5. To record again, select **Unmute recording**.
6. To copy the text, select **Copy transcript**. To close the panel, select **Collapse transcript**.
7. To stop, select **Stop recording**.

## What to expect

- The live transcript needs audio and an internet connection.
- The live transcript can be less polished than the final transcript. Ergo processes the final transcript after you stop.
- Missing audio can make the transcript incomplete.

## Common issues

- The transcript stays empty: check the microphone and screen recording permissions. Check your internet connection.
- Some speech is missing: the recording was muted, or an audio input was missing.
- The recording did not start or stop normally: you may not get a complete transcript.
