Use this page when a desktop recording did not start, did not upload or did not process.

## Symptoms

- Ergo did not show the card **Meeting detected**.
- The home page has no recording card with a timer under **Now**.
- A row in **Recent** shows **Processing failed**.
- A meeting card shows **Upload failed**.
- The meeting does not show in **Meetings**.

## Causes and fixes

1. **Ergo did not find the meeting.** Open **Settings → Desktop app → System permissions** in the desktop app. Make sure that **Accessibility** and **Screen Recording** show **Granted**. If a permission shows **Required**, grant it and select **Restart Ergo**. Ergo finds meetings in apps such as Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. For other conversations, select **Quick Note**, then **Record**.
2. **You did not start the recording.** There is no setting for automatic recording. When the card **Meeting detected** shows, select **Start Recording**. During a recording, the home page shows the recording card with a timer under **Now**.
3. **Processing is not done.** Look at **Recent** on the app home page. A row that shows **Processing...** is still in work. Wait.
4. **Processing failed.** Select **Retry** on the row that shows **Processing failed**. If Ergo shows "Failed to retry processing. Please try again.", contact Ergo support. See [Get support](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/2708243049).
5. **The upload failed.** Keep the app open and connected to the internet until the upload ends.
6. **A bot also recorded the call.** On the web, open **Meetings** and look for a recording of the same meeting from the Ergo Notetaker bot.

## Limits and good practice

- Ergo Desktop is for macOS. Download it from **Settings → User integrations → Note takers → Ergo Desktop Notetaker → Download**.
- One person can upload 40 recordings per hour.
- Do a test recording on the same computer before an important call.
- Close heavy apps before you record, especially when you share your screen.
- A very short recording can fail to give a useful transcript.
