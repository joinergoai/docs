The Ergo Desktop settings are on one page in the desktop app. You can also check for updates there.

## Who can use this

- Each person who uses Ergo Desktop.
- The page **Desktop app** shows only in the desktop app, not in the browser.

## Before you start

- Sign in to the Ergo desktop app. See [Install and sign in to Ergo Desktop](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4290838215).

## Steps

1. In the Ergo desktop app, open **Settings → Desktop app**. You can also press Cmd+,.
2. In **System permissions**, make sure that **Accessibility**, **Microphone** and **Screen Recording** show **Granted**.
3. In **Recording**, set **Video recording**. Turn it off to record audio only.
4. In **Recording**, set **Recording disclosure**. It is always on while **Video recording** is on.
5. In the section "Preferences", set **Launch at login**.
6. In **About**, select **Check for updates**. If the button changes to **Restart to update**, select it.
7. To sign out, open the user menu at the bottom of the left sidebar. Select **Log out**.

## What to expect

- The app cannot restart to update during a recording.
- The home page can also show a banner with **Restart to Update**.
- The user menu shows the name and email of the signed-in person.
- After **Log out**, you must sign in through the browser again.
- There is no setting for automatic recording. When Ergo finds a meeting on your computer, the home page shows the card **Meeting detected**. Select **Start Recording**.

## Common issues

- The app shows the wrong person: select **Log out** and sign in again.
- A permission does not show **Granted**: see [macOS permissions](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4584568980).
- A device that your company manages can block updates or permissions. Ask your IT team.
