Ergo Desktop records a meeting on your computer. No bot joins the call. By default it records video and audio.

## Who can use this

- Each person who records calls from their own computer.

## Before you start

- Install Ergo Desktop and sign in. See [Install and sign in to Ergo Desktop](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4290838215).
- Give the macOS permissions. See [macOS permissions](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4584568980).
- Check if a cloud bot is also scheduled for the same meeting.

## Steps

1. Open the Ergo desktop app before the meeting starts.
2. For a meeting on your calendar, find it under **Now** on the home page. Select **Join and Record** to open the meeting link and record. Select **Record** to record only.
3. For a meeting without a calendar event, select **Start Recording** on the card **Meeting detected**. Or select **Quick Note**, then **Record**.
4. Make sure that the recording card shows the timer and the label **Video** or **Audio only**.
5. Keep the app open during the call.
6. When the call ends, select **Stop recording** (the square icon).
7. In **Recent**, wait until **Processing...** goes away. Then open the meeting.

## What to expect

- Ergo records one meeting at a time. You cannot start a second recording until you stop the first.
- With **Video recording** on, Ergo posts a recording disclosure in the meeting chat of supported meetings.
- To record audio only, open **Settings → Desktop app → Recording** in the desktop app. Turn off **Video recording**. Then you can also turn off **Recording disclosure**.
- You must still follow the recording consent rules that apply to your meeting.

## Common issues

- The row in **Recent** shows **Processing failed**: select **Retry**.
- The upload stopped: keep the app open and connected to the internet until the upload ends.
- A cloud bot also recorded the call: you have two recordings of one meeting.
