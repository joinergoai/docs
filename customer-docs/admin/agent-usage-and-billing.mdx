**Settings** → **Usage** shows the included agent usage of each person and any extra usage in this billing cycle. It is a monitor. It is not an invoice.

## Who can use this

- Only an **Owner** can open **Settings** → **Usage**.

## Before you start

- For questions about your contract, seats, rates, or invoices, speak to your Ergo contact.

## Steps

1. Sign in as an Owner.
2. Open **Settings** → **Usage**.
3. Read the dates at the top: **Billing cycle** and **Credit period**.
4. Read **Extra usage this billing cycle**, **People over included usage**, and **Last billing cycle**.
5. In the table, find a person. Read **Included usage used** and **Extra usage**.
6. Look for the badge **Limit reached**. It shows that the person used all the included usage.
7. To see your own usage, click your name at the bottom of the left navigation. Select **Usage remaining**, if your organization has it.

## What to expect

- **Included usage used** is the percent of the included usage that the person used.
- **Extra usage** is the amount above the included usage.
- For **Extra usage this billing cycle**, the page gives the date of the invoice that has this amount.
- The page uses the billing cycle and the credit period, not the calendar month.
- If there is no extra usage, the page shows **$0.00** and "No extra usage yet. Everyone is within their included usage."

## Common issues

- **The page says "Usage isn't turned on for your organization."** Your organization does not have usage billing. Ask your Ergo contact.
- **The page says "Usage isn't available right now. Try again in a few minutes."** Click **Try again**.
- **You do not see Usage in Settings.** Only an Owner sees it.
- **The numbers do not match an invoice.** Use your contract and invoice for billing. Use this page to monitor.
