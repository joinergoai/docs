An Owner can set the default for post-call summary emails. The default applies to each person who has the Ergo Notetaker connected.

## Who can use this

- An **Owner** sets the organization default. The Owner's own **Ergo Notetaker** must be connected.
- Each person can change the setting for their own meetings.

## Steps

1. Sign in as an Owner. Make sure that your own **Ergo Notetaker** is connected.
2. Open the **Dashboard**.
3. In the **Upcoming Meetings** card, click the **⋮** button (**Calendar settings**) at the right of **Add Bot to Meeting**.
4. Set **Send Summary Email** to on or off.
5. If it is on, set **Send Recording & Transcript** and **Internal Recipients Only**.
6. Read the message that shows how many people Ergo updated.
7. Do a test with one external meeting.

## What to expect

- **Send Summary Email** also changes the setting at once for each person who has the **Ergo Notetaker** connected. The screen says "Overrides all users (they can change it back)."
- **Send Recording & Transcript** adds the recording link to the email.
- **Internal Recipients Only** sends the email only to people in your company.
- Each switch saves at once. There is no **Save** button.
- Each person can change the setting: **Settings** → **User integrations** → **Note takers** → gear icon on **Ergo Notetaker** → **Your settings**.
- The setting does not change meeting visibility, reporting access, or the recording disclaimer.
- Private meetings do not include the recording link in the summary email.

## Common issues

- **The switches are missing.** Make sure that you are an Owner and that your own **Ergo Notetaker** is connected.
- **A person changed it back.** Each person can change their own setting.
- **No email was sent.** Make sure that the meeting was processed and that Ergo made a summary.
- **The recording link is missing.** Make sure that **Send Recording & Transcript** is on and that the meeting is not private.
