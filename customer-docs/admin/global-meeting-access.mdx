By default people see their own meetings, team Admins see their teams' meetings, and the Owner sees everything. If your whole organization should open any recording, turn on **Everyone can see all meetings**.

## Who can use this

- An **Owner** only. Team Admins and Members do not see **Settings** → **Meetings**.

## Steps

1. Sign in as an Owner.
2. Open **Settings** → **Meetings**.
3. In the **Access** group, turn on **Everyone can see all meetings**.
4. Tell people to refresh the **Meetings** page.

The text below the switch says: "When off, people see their own meetings plus any access you grant in their profile."

## What to expect

- Every person, on every seat type, can open every meeting in the organization. This includes Spectators.
- Individual **Meeting access** grants are not needed while the switch is on. The dialog of the person shows *Everyone can see all meetings*.
- **Private meetings stay private.** A meeting that is private, by hand or by a Meeting Privacy Rule, is visible only to its owner and attendees.

## Related settings

- **Allow internal attendees to delete the meeting** (**Access** group): when off, only an Owner of the organization can delete a meeting. When on, a person from your organization who was in the meeting can also delete it.
- **Privacy** → **Meeting Privacy Rules** → **Edit rules**: meetings with a keyword in the title, or with the people that you select, are private to their participants.
- **Labels**: the labels that Ergo gives to new meetings.
- **Pipeline access** is on a different page: **Settings** → **Organization** → **Pipeline access** → **Manage**. It sets which teams can see which CRM pipelines.
