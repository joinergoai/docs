The **Automations** switch controls whether Ergo works on a person's meetings, emails, and Slack conversations. When it is on, Ergo also updates the CRM.

## Who can use this

- An **Owner** can change **Automations** for each person.
- A team **Admin** can change it only for the people in the teams that the Admin manages. For other people the switch is disabled.
- A **Member** sees the page read-only.
- A person with a Standard seat can change the setting for their own account.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** → **Members**.
2. On the **Members** tab, find the person. Or, on the **Teams** tab, click the team to see its people.
3. Look at the **Automations** switch in the row of the person.
4. To turn it on, click the switch. The person must have a Standard seat.
5. To turn it off, click the switch. Then click **Turn off Automations** in the confirmation.
6. To change your own account, open **Settings** → **Profile** → **Ergo on your account**. Use **Process my meetings, emails and Slack**.

## What to expect

- When the switch is on, Ergo processes the meetings, emails, and Slack conversations of the person and updates the CRM.
- A person with a Notetaker seat or a Spectator seat cannot have **Automations** on. On the **Teams** tab the **Automations** column shows **N/A** for these people.
- In the dialog of the person, the row shows "Not available on a Spectator seat." or "Not available on a Notetaker seat."

## Common issues

- **The switch is disabled.** You are not an Owner or an Admin of that person's team. The screen says "Only the admin of their team can turn Ergo on or off."
- **You cannot turn it on.** The person is on a Notetaker or Spectator seat. An Owner must change the seat first. See [Change a role or seat type](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/9544077862).
- **A person needs the CRM updated.** An Owner or a team Admin connects the CRM in **Settings** → **Org integrations** → **CRM**.
