Use this page for people who already have an Ergo account. To make an account, see [Provision accounts](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/4601303031).

## Who can use this

- Every action on this page is for an **Owner**.
- A team **Admin** can see the team but cannot add existing people to a team, move them, change their primary team, or remove them. Ask your Owner or Ergo support.

## Add a person to another team

1. Open **Settings** → **Members**, click the **Members** tab, and click the person.
2. In **Memberships**, click **Add to team**.
3. Select the **Team** and the **Role**, then click **Add to team**.

The person keeps the current teams. Admins of the new team can now see the person's meetings.

## Move a person or change the primary team

1. Open the person in **Settings** → **Members**.
2. Add the new team first. A person must always belong to at least one team.
3. In **Memberships**, click the **…** menu of a team. Select **Make primary team** or **Remove from team**.

**Make primary team** is disabled if the person is in only one team. **Remove from team** applies at once.

## Remove a person from the organization

1. Open the person in **Settings** → **Members**.
2. In the **Danger zone**, click **Remove**.
3. Type the person's email address and click **Remove from organization**.

## What to expect

- You cannot remove a person who is an owner. For an added owner, first remove the owner access: **Settings** → **Organization** → **Organization owners** → **Manage**.
- Only Ergo support can change the main owner.
- You cannot remove a person from their only team. Move the person to a different team first, or remove the person from the organization.
- You cannot remove the only Admin of a team. Ergo shows "Team "<name>" must have at least one active Admin." Make a different person an Admin of that team first.
- If a membership has the label **Managed by Okta**, it comes from your directory sync. To remove the person from that team, remove the person from the Okta group. An Owner can change the role in Ergo.
- To add the person back later, contact Ergo support. For a leave or a temporary change, change the seat or remove the person from a team.
