Set roles and seats on the person in **Settings** → **Members**. Only an Owner can make these changes.

## Who can use this

- Only an **Owner**. A team **Admin** sees the controls greyed out.
- An Owner can also change the Owner's own seat: **Settings** → **Profile** → **Access** → **Seat**.

## Change a role

A person is an **Admin** or a **Member** in each team. An Admin can edit the team, add new people to it, turn Automations on or off for its members, and edit whose meetings they can see.

1. Open **Settings** → **Members** and click the person.
2. In **Memberships**, use the role list next to the team. Select **Admin** or **Member**.

Ergo saves the change at once. A role change does not change the seat or the meeting access of the person.

Each team must have at least one Admin. Before you change the only Admin of a team to Member, make a different person an Admin of that team.

## Change a seat type

1. Open **Settings** → **Members** and click the person.
2. Change **Seat type**. Read the confirmation. It gives a short summary.
3. Click **Change seat**.

### What a downgrade turns off

| Cleanup | Standard → Notetaker | Standard or Notetaker → Spectator |
|---|---|---|
| Email features (inbox monitoring, triage, follow-ups, reply drafts, signature) | Turned off | Turned off |
| Third-party notetakers (Gong, Fireflies, Fathom, Grain, Granola, Read.ai, Circleback, BlueDot) | Disconnected | Disconnected |
| Own email and calendar connection | Kept | Disconnected |
| Personal Slack connection | Kept | Disconnected (the organization Slack app stays) |
| Ergo notetaker and scheduled recordings | Kept | Disconnected and cancelled |
| Automations | Turned off | Turned off |

Ergo does not delete anything that it recorded before.

### What an upgrade does

An upgrade to Standard or Notetaker changes the seat only. It does not connect again what Ergo disconnected. Tell the person to open **Settings** → **User integrations** and connect email and calendar, Slack, and the note taker again. Then make sure that the **Automations** switch of the person is on.

## Common issues

- **The Seat or Role control is greyed out.** You are a team Admin, not an Owner.
- **The message says the integration cleanup could not be started.** Click **Retry** in the message. You can also do the same seat change again.
- **The person cannot see Chat with Ergo.** Chat shows for Standard seats only.
- **The person lost meetings after a downgrade.** A Spectator sees only meetings that they attended or that you granted. See [Grant meeting or reporting access](https://support.joinergo.com/articles/3277963384).
